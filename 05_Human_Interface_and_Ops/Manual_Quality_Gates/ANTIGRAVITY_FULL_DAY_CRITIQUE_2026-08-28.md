# Antigravity Triton Corp Full-Day Critique — 2026-08-28

## Scope

This review covers all six Antigravity commits dated 2026-08-28, from `a225e29879b291d639d5e7c6bba807727a959101` through `4267af75a9a55c03fc22da3e4af943f2d1258204`: 79 unique changed paths and 16 atomic capability decisions. The Antigravity repository was inspected read-only and no external code was executed.

## Overall Assessment

The work has strong practical instincts. It tries to connect governance with runnable mechanisms, make human limitations visible, turn lessons into prevention, test cross-component flow, expose cost and decision data, and restore creativity to an otherwise rule-heavy Company OS. These are useful directions and should not be discarded.

The recurring weakness is overclaiming. Many methods are given universal numbers, automatic decisions or assurance labels without a declared source, task class, jurisdiction, baseline or competent authority. Several tools calculate a hash or pass a fixture and then name the result as approval, notarisation, reliability, Pareto optimality or compliance. That wording is materially stronger than the evidence.

## Capability Findings

### Governance, exceptions and decisions

The three-tier decision idea and bounded emergency path are useful. Fixed `WIP=1`, universal expedite percentages and automatic stoppage are not. Capacity and exceptions depend on the actual constraint, obligations and service evidence. Triton's existing adaptive harness and engineering-flow controls already provide the safer version.

### Commercial changes and disputes

Structured change capsules, evidence-led classification and reconciliation are valuable. Universal rush fees, invoice/deposit thresholds, automatic tax transfers, and generic BGB/VOB entitlement statements are not portable. Triton implemented a controlled change/exception record while preserving human commercial and legal authority.

### Requirements and contracts

Examples, teach-back and explicit acceptance evidence reduce misunderstanding. A keyword list cannot decide that a requirement is invalid, and AST structure equality cannot prove behavioural compatibility. These mechanisms should remain advisory or become contract-specific validators with representative fixtures.

### Built environment and delivery packages

Geometry-to-cost traceability, explicit allowances and package completeness are useful. Embedded prices, exchange rates, DIN categories, geometry corridors and automatic handover approval are unsafe as generic Company OS defaults. The capability remains retained behind project-owned adapters, dated sources and qualified review.

### Resilience and human support

Recovery evidence, constrained-resource buffers and user-initiated low-energy assistance are worth retaining. Universal slack/reserve percentages, fixed daily sprint caps, streak pressure, monetary self-rewards and neurological claims are unsuitable for an operating control. Triton adopted one-step low-energy support without diagnosis, surveillance or lower quality gates.

### Task investment and sourcing

Make/buy thinking and sunk-cost awareness are useful, but aversion, joy and a fixed `1.5x` rule cannot automatically delegate work or choose a supplier. Triton's existing Task Investment and Sourcing record covers obligations, readiness, lifecycle TCO, uncertainty and human decision more completely.

### Closed-loop learning

Turning incidents into prevention is valuable. A single incident must not automatically create a normative RAG rule. Root cause, recurrence, generality, domain applicability and independent acceptance are required. Triton retains the intent through product-owned incident evidence and sanitised reviewed learning exports.

### Corporate finance

NPV comparison and realised-outcome review are useful corporate capabilities. A universal `12%` MARR, automatic shutdown and fixed private wealth allocation are not. Triton implemented sourced corporate NPV and post-audit variance calculations without private financial advice or automatic investment action.

### Tool and MCP routing

Minimum-sufficient tool selection is useful. Keyword routing to named tools can bypass admission, permissions and data boundaries, while silent fallback can create unapproved egress. Triton implemented fail-closed routing only among independently admitted capabilities, with explicit fallback.

### Creativity and execution modes

Human anchors, briefing challenges, SCAMPER/reframing candidates and divergent exploration are valuable. The submitted TSDE ranking is not a Pareto front, aesthetic preferences are not universal constraints, and inferred unconscious needs are not evidence. Triton implemented real non-dominance, hard admissibility, `RULE_BOUND` and `EXPLORATORY` modes and a human convergence decision.

The exact “eight heuristics” and “WKW” method were not named or sourced in the supplied description. They remain an explicit evidence request rather than being invented or silently dropped.

### Testing and dashboards

Cross-engine acceptance tests and in-context KPI explanations are useful. The stress suite does not exercise its claimed concurrency/high-load properties, and the dashboards do not identify live data sources or status evidence. Triton implemented a hermetic cross-component path and an accessible source-bound dashboard renderer. Live operational dashboards still require approved data contracts.

## Recommendation to Antigravity

Keep the practical capabilities, but reduce the certainty of the claims. For every formula, threshold, automatic action and green status, state its source, context, evidence consumer, decision authority and invalidation trigger. Separate calculation from approval, candidate from release, hash from notarisation, test pass from reliability, and creative ranking from Pareto analysis. Submit the next version as small capability slices with negative cases and exact data contracts rather than another broad package claiming universal readiness.
