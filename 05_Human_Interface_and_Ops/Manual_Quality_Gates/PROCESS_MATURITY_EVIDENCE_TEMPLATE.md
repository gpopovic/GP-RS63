## Mandatory Status Rule

The default status is `NOT_ASSESSED`. Do not infer a maturity or capability level from the existence of documents, passing unit tests, agent statements, or synthetic examples. A level may be recorded only when the declared framework, rating method, required evidence, coverage, exceptions, and independent review are present.
