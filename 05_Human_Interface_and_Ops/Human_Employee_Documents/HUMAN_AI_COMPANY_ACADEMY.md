# Triton Human–AI Company Academy

## Goal

Enable people to operate Triton confidently: give agents bounded tasks, inspect evidence, make accountable decisions, and improve the system without treating AI output as authority. The Academy is useful for the founder, new employees, team leads, and human AI Pod Stewards.

## Foundation Path: Every Human Colleague

| Module | Outcome | Demonstration of learning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Company map and boundaries | Identify Company OS versus product workspace, data classes, approvals, and escalation paths | Classify a sample request and choose the permitted workspace and owner |
| 2. Roles and meetings | Read a role, role engagement, decision record, and meeting output | Assign Actor, Challenger, Validator, Chair, and accountable human to a scenario |
| 3. Task packets and evidence | Turn an intention into inputs, acceptance criteria, constraints, output, and evidence | Prepare a work packet and reject an unsupported agent answer |
| 4. Quality, challenge, and escalation | Use independent review, deterministic checks, and a quality gate | Run a bounded disagreement and create a decision or escalation record |
| 5. Agile and ALM practice | Work from a lean backlog through SAFe-aligned planning and evidence | Create a Shadow Backlog item with Definition of Ready and Done |
| 6. Safe improvement | Propose a controlled role, model, skill, or process change | Complete a change proposal with KPI, risk, test, rollback, and review date |

## AI Pod Steward Path

For people who coordinate several agents, this additional path covers workload-aware pod design, delegation levels, independent sampling, evidence calibration, model-routing limits, privacy boundaries, conflict facilitation, and stop-and-escalate decisions.

The qualification exercise is a supervised case: configure a small producer–challenger–validator pod, explain which outcome may be trusted and why, identify a failure, pause the delegation, and record the corrective action. Passing means the reviewer can reproduce the evidence trail and accepts the decision logic.

## Founder First Run

Start with a synthetic, low-risk feature or offer scenario. Read its work packet, call the relevant meeting, compare the producer and challenger positions, inspect the validator evidence, and either make a recorded decision or escalate. Do not begin with live customer, personnel, legal, or production data.

## Training Records

Record learner, path, scenario version, assessor, result, observed gaps, remediation, renewal date, and any accessibility or language support. Training records demonstrate capability; they must not become behavioural surveillance.
