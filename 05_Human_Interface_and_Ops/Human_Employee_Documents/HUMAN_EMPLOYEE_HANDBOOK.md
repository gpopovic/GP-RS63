# Triton AI Corp Human Employee Handbook

## Purpose

This handbook explains how human colleagues work inside Triton's hybrid operating system. It translates the governed Company OS into practical expectations. The canonical Markdown role instructions remain the controlled source for agent runtime; this handbook is the human-facing operating guide.

## How Your Role Works

Your job title, team assignment, and role engagement are recorded for each meaningful assignment. A canonical role defines competence, authority, boundaries, and expected results. It does not restrict you to a single activity.

You may be assigned as an Actor, Advisor, Challenger, Validator, Auditor, Panel Member, or Chair. Every assignment states its scope, inputs, expected output, decision rights, time boundary, and escalation owner.

## Independence and Speaking Up

You may challenge a weak claim, propose an improvement, and defend correct work with evidence. Record material disagreement in the relevant decision record; unresolved conflict follows the bounded deliberation and escalation process.

You may not be the sole independent validator, auditor, or release authority for work you designed, executed, or commercially own. Disclose conflicts of interest early. If separation is difficult, use separate assignments, independent evidence review, and proportionate external review.

## Cross-Functional Feature Teams

Teams form around a customer or product outcome, not departmental queues. A typical feature team contains Product Owner, Requirements Engineer, Architect, delivery specialists, Tester or Quality specialist, and Scrum Master. Business, cost-benefit, legal, security, privacy, procurement, and domain specialists join when the feature requires them.

For construction or built-environment work, the team activates Civil Engineering, Structural Engineering, Architecture, Interior or Exterior Design, cost estimation, and independent site inspection as relevant.

The Product Owner orders the backlog. The Scrum Master enables team effectiveness and productive events. The team creates the increment and its evidence. Independent quality roles evaluate defined gates.

## From Idea to Release

1. Capture the signal or idea with source, problem, affected users, expected outcome, and known constraints.
2. Use Feature Investment Review for material options. The review examines evidence, alternatives, benefit, cost range, risks, privacy, compliance, feasibility, dependencies, success measure, and stop condition.
3. The Product Owner decides whether and when to order the validated option in the backlog.
4. The feature team defines acceptance criteria, delivery plan, evidence needs, and a usable increment.
5. Deliver, test, inspect, and release through the applicable quality gates.
6. Export approved aggregate KPIs and lessons learned for future improvement. Product work itself remains in its external product workspace.

## ALM and Jira

Jira or another ALM system is the single operational source of truth for a work item. Use the smallest required set of fields, a visible definition of ready and done, linked evidence, named ownership, and dated dependencies.

Before an approved Jira/ALM tenant is available, use the controlled Shadow Backlog protocol instead. SAFe governs scaling and cross-team escalation; Nexus is used only as an optional shared-product integration practice and cannot override an approved SAFe decision.

Request a new field, workflow state, automation, statistic, chart, or dashboard only when it answers a named decision question. The ALM and Jira Product Administrator pilots material changes with the affected teams, measures their administrative burden and data quality, and keeps rollback evidence.

## Meetings and Communication

Use asynchronous updates and a decision record by default. Schedule a meeting only when a decision, conflict, risk, design session, or collaboration task cannot be resolved asynchronously.

Every meeting needs a purpose, required inputs, decision owner, timebox, and recorded outputs. Outputs include decisions, owners, due dates, risks, assumptions, and escalations. The Scrum Master and Communication Operations function remove low-value meetings and improve communication flow.

Communication KPIs are normally evaluated at team or process level: decision latency, meeting decision yield, clarification loops, hand-off completeness, interruption load, and improvement effect. They are not individual surveillance or a raw activity target.

## KPI and Improvement Loop

Each role extracts the approved KPIs relevant to its work. Report the value and period, data source or evidence, definition, confidence, observed cause or hypothesis, risk of inaction, and suggested improvement or escalation.

The responsible optimisation capability reviews the signal. Quality and process signals go to Quality Management and Continuous Improvement; communication signals to Communication Operations and Scrum Masters; commercial signals to Business and Finance; product signals to Product Improvement and the Product Owner; role, skill, model, and MCP signals to the Role Skill Evolution process.

Never alter a record, metric, or evidence item merely to improve a reported result. A speed or cost gain is not a success if quality, safety, legal, security, privacy, traceability, customer value, or financial risk worsens.

## Working With AI Agents

An AI agent is a bounded contributor, never the accountable decision maker. For a material work package, name a human accountable owner, the producing role, an independent challenger or reviewer, the validator, and the human gate owner. Do not ask one agent to design, validate, and approve its own work.

AI Pod Stewards may coordinate several agents only after completing the relevant Academy path and demonstrating that they can inspect evidence, pause unsafe delegation, and escalate uncertainty. A reported 95% result is valid only for its defined role, task class, model/tool configuration, data boundary, and evaluation period. It does not transfer automatically to high-risk work.

Use the Foundation Path in `HUMAN_AI_COMPANY_ACADEMY.md` before operating a live team. Team leads and AI Pod Stewards complete the additional supervised qualification.

## Company Configuration Management

Roles, meetings, KPIs, ALM configurations, skills, MCPs, model routing, and process models are controlled Company OS configuration items. Material changes require a documented reason, evidence, impact assessment, owner, test or trial, decision, effective date, review date, and rollback route.

Do not silently overwrite an approved role, workflow, metric, control, or model configuration. Raise a change proposal through the relevant owner when improvement is needed.

## Your First Week Checklist

- Read your assigned canonical role instruction and the Collaboration Constitution.
- Confirm your current team, role engagement, decision rights, escalation route, and conflict-of-interest status.
- Learn the team backlog, Definition of Done, quality gates, ALM workflow, and current priorities.
- Agree communication channels, meeting cadence, asynchronous update convention, and evidence location.
- Review the relevant KPI definitions and how to submit improvement signals.
- Ask early when evidence, authority, responsibility, or acceptance criteria are unclear.
