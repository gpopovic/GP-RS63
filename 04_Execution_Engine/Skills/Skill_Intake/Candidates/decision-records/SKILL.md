# Decision Records

## Workflow

1. Give the decision a unique title and state the decision question.
2. Record context, constraints, decision criteria, evidence, and realistic alternatives.
3. State the chosen option, rationale, consequences, dissent, owner, and implementation or review trigger.
4. Link to supporting artifacts without duplicating their content.
5. Mark the record proposed, accepted, superseded, or rejected; never rewrite historical rationale silently.

## Output

Use compact Markdown headings: `Context`, `Decision Drivers`, `Considered Options`, `Decision Outcome`, `Consequences`, `Evidence`, `Owner and Review`.
