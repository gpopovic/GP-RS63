## Promoted Improvements

1. **Innovation maturity path.** The Responsible Innovation governance now specifies Potential Analysis, Lab Small, Lab Full, Field Study, and Roll-out with evidence and stop/advance decisions. It preserves the approved project data boundaries at every stage.
2. **AI dependency resilience.** `AI_DEPENDENCY_AND_RESILIENCE_POLICY.md` now requires a dependency register, evidenced fallback testing, bounded routing, configuration control, and proportionate quality, security, privacy, and cost review.
3. **Delivery-cell continuity and focus.** Managed delivery cells now require a named deputy or equivalent continuity route. Cross-border working agreements make overlap, hand-off, escalation, and protected focus explicit.
4. **Human–AI apprenticeship.** A new policy protects the ability to challenge and validate AI outputs through supervised practice, competence evidence, and reusable learning cases.
5. **Controlled remediation.** Automated monitoring may diagnose and prepare a repair proposal; production repair remains subject to tests, impact review, rollback evidence, and the approved release decision.
