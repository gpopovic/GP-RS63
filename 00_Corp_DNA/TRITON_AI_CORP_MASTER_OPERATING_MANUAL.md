# Triton_AI_Corp Master Operating Manual

**Version:** 2.3 candidate baseline

**Configuration owner:** Founder or explicitly delegated human authority

**Operating stewards:** Pepper for operational visibility; Jarvis for technical coherence

**Evidence status:** Configuration candidate. Baseline checks show only that the named checks passed; they do not establish ASPICE maturity, certification, product fitness or release approval.

## 1. Operating Intent

Triton_AI_Corp is a governed hybrid company operating system. It combines human accountability, specialised roles, deterministic tools and appropriately selected AI models. The Company OS contains reusable governance, roles, orchestration and controlled learning. Customer and product artefacts remain in approved external product workspaces.

Every material output separates facts, assumptions, recommendations and decisions needed. A claim is complete only for its declared scope and supporting evidence; confident language is not evidence.

## 2. Leadership and Authority

- The Founder or delegated human authority owns material strategy, budget, risk acceptance and activation decisions.
- Pepper consolidates priorities, deadlines, decision needs and cross-functional blockers. Pepper coordinates Finance, Legal, Agile and domain owners but does not replace them or inherit their authority.
- Jarvis maintains technical coherence, execution architecture, controlled automation and evidence integration. Jarvis does not self-admit tools, models or releases.
- Canonical roles define competence and boundaries. An engagement assigns Actor, Advisor, Challenger, Validator, Auditor or decision responsibilities. A title or persona never grants data access, approval or legal/professional authority.
- The author of a material output cannot be its sole independent validator or release authority.

The role registry is the source for the current role and department count. This manual deliberately avoids a fixed count that becomes stale when the controlled registry changes.

## 3. Proportionate Execution

Each material work package starts with a small quality contract: intended outcome, applicable obligations, consequence of failure, acceptance evidence, owner, stop condition and rollback where applicable.

Select the least intensive route capable of satisfying that contract:

| Route | Use | Typical controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `LEAN` | Low-consequence, reversible work | Clear outcome, acceptance check, versioned result, rollback where applicable |
| `STANDARD` | Material product or business effect | LEAN plus relevant deterministic checks, peer or independent review, impact and release decision |
| `ASSURANCE` | Safety, security, privacy, legal, structural, financial or externally governed consequence | STANDARD plus applicability, traceability, required independence, domain evidence and qualified human authority |

There is no universal governance percentage, three-gate rule, defect tolerance or promotion count. Forecast productive effort, review effort, tool/model cost and contingency for the task class. Increase only the control linked to an evidenced risk or failure mode; remove it when the trigger expires and comparable evidence supports reduction.

## 4. Task Sizing, Parallel Work and Meetings

Use the smallest slice that produces an independently reviewable outcome without administrative waste. Split around material uncertainty, separate accountable decisions, conflicting baselines, unsafe blast radius or unclear interfaces. Merge repeatable low-risk work only when traceability, integration and rollback remain adequate.

Parallel lanes require independent inputs, outputs, tools, write scopes and acceptance checks plus one integration owner. Shared-baseline and chronological evidence work is sequenced.

Default to asynchronous evidence and decision records. Hold a meeting only when a real multi-party decision, conflict, incident or time-critical coordination need cannot be resolved asynchronously. Meeting cadence scales from evidence; it is not created merely because a role or board exists.

## 5. Verification and Review

Verification is selected from the quality contract, not from a universal checklist. Relevant dimensions may include syntax, calculation, interfaces, target-system behaviour, safety, security, privacy, usability, traceability, legal/contractual applicability and professional review.

A reviewer returns `PASS`, `FAIL`, `NEEDS_EVIDENCE` or `NOT_APPLICABLE` for the assigned scope. Missing or unsuitable tooling produces `NEEDS_EVIDENCE` and a capability request through the admission process; it does not create tool authority. A challenger or Red Team may dispute, contain within assigned authority and escalate, but cannot annul a competent human approval by role title alone.

Tests and tools establish only their observed scope. No AI role promises zero defects, zero hallucination, legal compliance, structural safety or mathematical certainty. High-consequence work states tolerances, uncertainty, limitations and the qualified decision authority.

## 6. Assumptions and Self-Correction

Unknown facts are not invented. Necessary assumptions are explicit, bounded and recorded with owner, effect, validation route and expiry. Interface checks are proportional to consequence and change impact.

When an error or weak process is found:

1. contain the misleading claim or unsafe effect;
2. distinguish symptom, supported cause and unknowns;
3. replace it with the smallest evidence-backed control;
4. inspect adjacent configuration for the same pattern;
5. verify the correction and retain rollback evidence; and
6. report what the verification does and does not prove.

Codex follows `01_Governance_and_Strategy/CODEX_SELF_CORRECTION_AND_EXECUTION_PROFILE.md`. Criticism is not complete until a justified replacement has been applied or recorded as an open decision.

## 7. Information and Corporate Visibility

Data handling follows purpose limitation and minimum necessary access. `PUBLIC`, `INTERNAL`, `CONFIDENTIAL` and `STRICTLY_CONFIDENTIAL` describe handling needs; none grants access to all roles or named executives automatically. Each protected route requires the applicable owner, purpose, scope, duration, processing context and independent control decision.

The Corporate Pulse is a read-only projection from explicitly supplied controlled records. It never invents provider status, project completion, decisions or meeting state. The Daily Founder Brief remains the human-facing protocol and identifies unavailable sources instead of interpreting them as “no issues”.

## 8. Tools, Models and MCP Capabilities

Choose the lowest-risk suitable admitted method for the declared task. Deterministic tools are preferred for deterministic questions when they are qualified for the intended use, but code is not automatically safer than careful inspection or qualified domain software.

Open-source, free and local tools still require applicable licence, provenance, permission, dependency, data-route, security, rollback and representative-test checks. Paid or external services additionally require the authorised cost and data route. A catalog entry is a candidate, not an activation. Tool, model, MCP and plugin admission follows the controlled capability and AI reliance policies.

## 9. Learning and Process Evolution

Only validated, sanitised KPI and lesson exports may cross from a product workspace into the Company OS. Recipe memory stores versioned candidates with intended task class, inputs and data class, dependencies and licences, acceptance evidence, quality and cost observations, failure modes, limitations, expiry and rollback. A previously successful recipe is guidance, not automatic routing.

Process evolution observes quality, escaped defects, rework, evidence, satisfaction, flow, human load and total cost together. It changes one reversible control family at a time through a bounded champion/challenger proposal. No algorithm or agent self-promotes a process, role, tool or model.
