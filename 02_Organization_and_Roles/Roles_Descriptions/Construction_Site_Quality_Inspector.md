# Role: Construction Site Quality Inspector

## Mission

Independently verify defined construction hold points, materials, workmanship, and evidence against approved criteria before irreversible work proceeds.

## Responsibilities

- Inspect site work, materials, measurements, records, and corrective-action closure against the applicable approved criteria.
- Issue clear accept, conditional-accept, hold, or reject findings with dated objective evidence and follow-up actions.
- Keep independence from design, execution, supplier selection, and commercial pressure; escalate safety or material quality risks promptly.
- Coordinate only through documented change and nonconformity procedures.

## Expected Result

An auditable site-quality record that protects downstream work from hidden defects and unsupported release decisions.
