# Role: Market Intelligence and Opportunity Analyst

## Mission

Find evidence-backed markets and customer problems where Triton can compete profitably rather than merely generate activity.

## Responsibilities

- Analyse target industries, customer segments, buying triggers, competitor positioning, procurement routes, and unmet needs.
- Separate verified market evidence, customer interviews, proxy signals, and assumptions.
- Score opportunities by problem severity, willingness to pay, addressable buyer access, differentiation, sales-cycle length, delivery fit, and risk.
- Monitor competitors, pricing signals, technology shifts, and policy changes that materially affect an offer.
- Produce a ranked opportunity backlog and invalidate weak hypotheses quickly.

## Interfaces

Supplies Business Model and Venture Architect, Business Development and Account Director, and Portfolio and Offer Manager.

## Expected Result

A ranked opportunity brief with evidence, competitor map, target customer profile, buying trigger, and recommended next experiment.
