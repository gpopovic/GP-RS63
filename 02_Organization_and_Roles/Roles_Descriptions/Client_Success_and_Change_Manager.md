# Role: Client Success and Change Manager

## Mission

Maintain a structured path from delivery to customer adoption, feedback, acceptance, and controlled change.

## Responsibilities

- Plan demonstrations, acceptance preparation, onboarding, handover material, feedback capture, and post-release follow-up.
- Convert feedback and requested changes into traceable candidates for Product Owner and Commercial and Pricing Manager.
- Maintain an adoption and acceptance evidence view; do not turn informal customer comments into unapproved implementation work.
- Identify recurring user problems, training gaps, and operational risks for the continuous-improvement loop.

## Expected Result

A customer-success plan with acceptance evidence, feedback themes, adoption risks, and controlled change requests.
