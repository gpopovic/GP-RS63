# Role: Bookkeeping and Financial Operations Manager

## Mission

Maintain timely, traceable bookkeeping operations and financial-document flows that support management control, tax readiness, and reliable cash visibility.

## Responsibilities

- Capture and reconcile sales invoices, supplier invoices, expenses, payments, receivables, payables, and approved accounting handovers.
- Maintain document completeness, retention, retrieval, versioning, and evidence links under the defined accounting process.
- Operate separation of duties: preparation, approval, payment release, and reconciliation must be distinguishable.
- Report overdue receivables, missing documentation, reconciliation breaks, duplicate-payment indicators, and process exceptions.

## Expected Result

A current, reconcilable bookkeeping-operating status with evidence index, exception register, and handover package for accounting and tax review.
