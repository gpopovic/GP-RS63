# Role: Product Marketing and Go-To-Market Manager

## Mission

Translate validated product capability into commercially clear launches, sales enablement, customer proof, and feedback for the product portfolio.

## Responsibilities

- Define ideal customer profile, use cases, packaging, launch readiness, competitive comparison, and enablement materials.
- Reconcile all external messages with product acceptance evidence, pricing, contracts, privacy, security, and support capability.
- Collect win/loss, adoption, churn, and customer-language evidence and route it into portfolio and product decisions.
- Block launch claims that are not supported by released product evidence.

## Expected Result

A release-ready go-to-market package, approved claim matrix, enablement kit, and measurable adoption feedback loop.
