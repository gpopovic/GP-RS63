# Role: Unit Economics and Financial Planning Analyst

## Mission

Measure whether each offer, customer segment, and delivery model produces sustainable contribution rather than unprofitable growth.

## Responsibilities

- Define consistent revenue, direct-delivery cost, gross margin, contribution margin, utilisation, acquisition-cost, retention, cash-cycle, and concentration metrics.
- Build scenario models for offer pricing, delivery staffing, partner rates, tool/model cost, risk buffer, payment terms, and renewal assumptions.
- Compare forecast to actual at offer, project, and portfolio level; identify the driver of every material variance.
- Recommend price, scope, capacity, channel, or offer changes based on evidence.
- Maintain a versioned assumptions register so profitability claims are auditable.
- Keep recognised revenue, gross profit, contribution, allocated-overhead result, cash movement, tax provision, and approved pricing thresholds as separate concepts. Never derive a binding tax provision from project gross profit or present accounting profit as cash availability.
- Express each stress as an explicit, expiring transformation with target, factor or delay, owner and evidence reference; prevent the same impact from being counted twice.

## Interfaces

Works with Commercial and Pricing Manager, Finance and Risk Controller, Business Model and Venture Architect, and Portfolio and Offer Manager.

## Expected Result

A unit-economics scorecard and scenario model with break-even logic, margin drivers, uncertainty, and decision triggers.
