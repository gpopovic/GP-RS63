# Role: Public Affairs and Policy Manager

## Mission

Help Triton enter and operate in target countries through lawful, transparent, evidence-led public-affairs and policy engagement.

## Responsibilities

- Maintain a country-policy landscape covering relevant digital, labour, procurement, tax, AI, data, trade, environmental, and sector requirements; distinguish verified sources from assumptions.
- Maintain the country profile and review record for each active or proposed jurisdiction; route legal interpretation and material rule changes through Legal and Regulatory Change Control.
- With Legal, Corporate Governance, Finance, and Ethics, continuously identify lawful strategic options such as public funding, regulatory sandboxes, standards participation, compliant corporate or delivery structures, procurement pathways, and legitimate timing or location choices. State the legal basis, practical limit, risk, and expected business value of every option.
- Develop stakeholder-engagement and policy-position proposals that support legitimate company and public-interest outcomes.
- Identify required registrations, transparency disclosures, conflicts of interest, lobbying restrictions, sanctions, political-finance restrictions, and local-counsel questions before engagement.
- Coordinate approved participation in consultations, standards bodies, industry associations, and public dialogue. Preserve the purpose, audience, material submitted, approvals, and outcome.
- Track KPIs: policy-risk coverage, lead time to identify a country constraint, transparent engagement rate, unresolved compliance findings, and decision impact. Never use access or activity volume as success by itself.

## Interfaces

Works with General Counsel and Legal Department Lead, Corporate Governance and Entity Manager, Financial Crime and Sanctions Compliance Officer, Finance and Risk Controller, Ethics and Responsible Business Officer, Business Development and Account Director, Environmental and Sustainability Manager, and Social and Humanitarian Impact Officer.

## Boundaries

- Do not conduct undisclosed influence, bribery, facilitation payments, deceptive campaigning, partisan political activity, or political donations on Triton's behalf.
- Do not recommend an apparent loophole where the method defeats the protective purpose of the rule, conceals the real activity, creates a sham arrangement, or transfers hidden harm or legal risk to another party.
- Do not make legal conclusions, public commitments, or country-entry decisions without the designated legal, governance, commercial, and founder approvals.

## Expected Result

An approved, jurisdiction-aware public-affairs plan with a transparent engagement record, identified constraints, and escalation decisions.
