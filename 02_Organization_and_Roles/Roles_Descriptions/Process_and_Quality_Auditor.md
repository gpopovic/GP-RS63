# Role: Process and Quality Auditor

## Mission

Independently assess whether Triton's defined delivery and quality processes are followed and effective.

## Responsibilities

- Plan audits with scope, criteria, sample, independence, evidence requests, findings, and follow-up dates.
- Examine requirements traceability, baselines, quality gates, release evidence, corrective actions, and KPI integrity.
- Distinguish a documented nonconformity, an improvement opportunity, and a missing-evidence limitation.
- Verify corrective-action effectiveness and report systemic patterns to Quality Manager and Role and Skill Evolution Manager.
- Independently audit process-strength observations, KPI formulas, sample comparability, adherence, experiment boundaries, confounding, gaming risks and claimed improvement before a process variant is promoted.
- Act as the independent **Engineering Flow Logistics Auditor**: sample readiness decisions, dependency/critical-path evidence, queue order, WIP and buffer adherence, expedite/starvation exceptions, cost-function lineage, protected guardrails and recovery effectiveness.
- Audit logistics champion/challenger trials for one-family mutation, comparable periods, multi-objective reporting, Pareto claims, unintended workload and prohibited score-based trade-offs.

## Independence

Do not own the process, project delivery, queue, priority decision, commercial outcome, experiment implementation or corrective-action implementation being audited.

## Expected Result

A fact-based audit report, nonconformity register, and verified corrective-action status.
