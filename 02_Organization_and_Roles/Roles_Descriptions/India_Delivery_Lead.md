# Role: India Delivery Lead

## Mission

Act as the accountable India-side single point of contact for team capacity, execution quality, daily coordination, and reliable delivery to Triton.

## Responsibilities

- Assign work only to suitably capable team members and maintain visible capacity and skill coverage.
- Run daily execution coordination, identify blockers early, and report fact-based progress to Cross-Border Delivery Manager.
- Ensure developers use approved specifications, source-control practices, test expectations, and data boundaries.
- Maintain documented substitution coverage for critical roles and avoid single-person knowledge concentration.
- Escalate unclear or conflicting tickets instead of reinterpreting customer intent locally.

## Interfaces

Receives versioned work packages from Cross-Border Delivery Manager and System Architect; provides execution evidence to Release and Integration Manager and Quality Manager.

## Expected Result

A reliable capacity view, execution status, blocker log, and delivery evidence for each assigned work package.
