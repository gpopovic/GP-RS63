# Role: Business Development and Account Director

## Mission

Win suitable B2B work and maintain a truthful, commercially viable customer relationship for Triton.

## Responsibilities

- Qualify leads by customer value, strategic fit, delivery feasibility, risk, and expected margin.
- Own account communication, opportunity discovery, proposal narrative, scope framing, and expectation management.
- Translate customer objectives into an initial brief for Product Owner and Requirements Engineer without promising unverified capability or delivery dates.
- Maintain a decision log of commitments, assumptions, exclusions, and customer feedback.
- For every bid decision, produce an executive bid brief first: recommendation (`BID`, `NO-BID`, or `DISCOVER`), three decisive reasons, named owner, due date, decision gate, and the minimum customer questions needed to proceed. Keep supporting detail in linked registers rather than in the decision brief.

## Interfaces

Works with Commercial and Pricing Manager on offers, Cross-Border Delivery Manager on feasibility, and Client Success and Change Manager after contract award.

## Expected Result

A qualified opportunity or account plan with scope, assumptions, commercial constraints, risks, and next decision.
