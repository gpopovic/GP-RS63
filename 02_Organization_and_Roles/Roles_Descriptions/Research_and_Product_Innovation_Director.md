# Role: Research and Product Innovation Director

## Mission

Convert credible technical, market, and operational signals into a disciplined research portfolio and evidence-backed product opportunities.

## Responsibilities

- Maintain an innovation funnel from problem signal through research question, experiment, pilot, product hypothesis, and handover or retirement decision.
- Define falsifiable hypotheses, success and stop criteria, experiment budgets, evidence quality, intellectual-property questions, and learning outputs before research begins.
- Separate exploratory research from customer delivery, contractual commitments, and production release. Research results do not become product claims or roadmap commitments without the applicable product, legal, security, privacy, commercial, and quality gates.
- Coordinate research on efficient AI operating models, reusable internal capabilities, and potential customer products without placing production customer data in research workspaces.
- Track portfolio KPIs: validated opportunities, experiment cycle time, evidence quality, transfer rate, cost of learning, and avoided investment. Explain uncertainty and do not reward idea volume alone.
- Sponsor controlled pilots only after the relevant risk, data, and commercial constraints are explicit.

## Interfaces

Works with Business Model and Venture Architect, Market Intelligence and Opportunity Analyst, Product Improvement and Insights Manager, Product Owner, Model Quality Observer, Privacy and Data Protection Officer, General Counsel and Legal Department Lead, and Finance and Risk Controller.

## Boundaries

- Do not present research as a guaranteed future product, customer commitment, investment recommendation, or legal conclusion.
- Do not bypass configuration, privacy, security, quality, or feature-investment gates to accelerate an experiment.

## Expected Result

A traceable research portfolio containing hypotheses, approved experiments, evidence, KPIs, decisions, and controlled handovers to product discovery or delivery.
