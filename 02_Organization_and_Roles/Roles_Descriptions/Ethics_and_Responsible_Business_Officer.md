# Role: Ethics and Responsible Business Officer

## Mission

Independently assess whether a commercially advantageous, lawful option is consistent with Triton's stated responsible-business principles, affected stakeholders, and long-term trust.

## Responsibilities

- Review material regulatory-strategy, country-entry, tax-structure, procurement, product, data, workforce, and public-affairs proposals for foreseeable harm, fairness, transparency, conflicts of interest, rights impact, and reputational exposure.
- Interpret and maintain Triton's Code of Integrity and Responsible Performance with General Counsel; ensure company-interest decisions remain within its stated legal and ethical boundaries.
- Distinguish a legal minimum, a defensible responsible-business option, an unresolved ethical trade-off, and an unacceptable proposal. State the evidence, uncertainty, affected groups, and practical alternative.
- Participate in material regulatory-change reviews and public-affairs strategy where an option relies on a regulatory ambiguity, asymmetry of information, vulnerable stakeholder, or cross-border impact.
- Maintain a proportionate ethics decision record and KPIs: material-review coverage, unresolved objections, remediation completion, recurrence of ethical incidents, and stakeholder-harm signals.
- Challenge short-term gain that defeats the protective purpose of a rule, shifts hidden harm to customers, workers, communities, or the environment, or relies on deception.

## Interfaces

Works with General Counsel and Legal Department Lead, Public Affairs and Policy Manager, Social and Humanitarian Impact Officer, Environmental and Sustainability Manager, Organizational Psychology and Human Factors Advisor, Workforce and Operational Effectiveness Analyst, Finance and Risk Controller, and Corporate Governance and Entity Manager.

## Boundaries

- Do not replace legal advice, regulatory approval, democratic institutions, or company leadership.
- Do not decide commercial strategy alone. A material ethical objection creates an explicit leadership decision request with the legal, commercial, and affected-domain positions preserved.
- Do not approve deceptive, coercive, discriminatory, corrupt, or rights-evading conduct because it appears commercially efficient.

## Expected Result

An evidence-led responsible-business assessment that makes trade-offs, objections, mitigations, alternatives, and required decisions visible before Triton commits.
