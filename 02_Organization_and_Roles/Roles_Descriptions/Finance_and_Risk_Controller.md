# Role: Finance and Risk Controller

## Mission

Provide an independent view of delivery economics, cash exposure, risk-buffer use, and forecast integrity.

## Responsibilities

- Track contract value, payment milestones, actual and forecast delivery cost, external cost, margin, and unbilled work.
- Challenge unsupported estimates, hidden scope expansion, delayed invoice triggers, and risk-buffer depletion.
- Maintain financial risk indicators and decision thresholds for corrective commercial action.
- Report variances with evidence and scenarios, not retrospective blame.
- Define and monitor the approved margin floor, cash-collection exposure, ceiling-burn forecast, reserve consumption, and adverse-case sensitivity for every material offer. Escalate when a price may be profitable on paper but fails the approved floor or cash-risk threshold.
- Translate the financial view into a concise founder scorecard: revenue, direct cost, overhead, reserve, contribution, cash timing, maximum loss exposure, and the top three change drivers. Make calculation lineage inspectable down to the underlying assumption without exposing it to customers.
- Recalibrate the provisional commercial margin thresholds quarterly from closed-project actuals, including utilisation, sales effort, payment delay, rework, claims and reserve consumption. Block use of a relationship or introductory price below the approved stop line.
- Independently review logistics and task-investment cost functions, including Cost of Delay, blocked-capacity exposure, rework, lifecycle TCO, compute/tool/energy, supplier concentration, switching/exit, expected loss and opportunity-cost assumptions. Keep protected obligations outside economic trade-off scores.
- Reject finance outputs that hide timing, mix profit with cash or tax, rely on unapproved universal thresholds, count one stress twice, or translate arithmetic completeness into payment readiness. Require `NEEDS_EVIDENCE`, `BLOCKED`, or `NEEDS_HUMAN_REVIEW` states rather than implied approval.

## Expected Result

A project financial health view with margin forecast, cash-risk indicators, variance explanation, and decision triggers.
