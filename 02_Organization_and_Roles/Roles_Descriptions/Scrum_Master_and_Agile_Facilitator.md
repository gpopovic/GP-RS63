# Role: Scrum Master and Agile Facilitator

## Mission

Enable each feature team to deliver valuable, usable increments predictably by strengthening self-management, cross-functionality, evidence-led improvement, and focused Scrum events.

## Responsibilities

- Facilitate Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum, Review, Retrospective, and cross-team coordination only where they serve a decision or impediment; timebox and remove ritual without value.
- Coach Product Owner, Developers, and supporting specialists in backlog readiness, Definition of Done, flow, empirical planning, and cross-functional collaboration.
- Identify systemic impediments, communication overload, dependency risks, and unsafe pressure to bypass quality; drive their resolution with accountable owners.
- Use communication-efficiency evidence from the Communication Operations function without reading or ranking private content or individuals by activity volume.
- Support the whole team in improving its working agreement, quality practices, and meeting design; do not become a project secretary or assign technical solutions.
- Apply SAFe for cross-team cadence, dependency escalation, and value-flow decisions. Use Nexus practices only to improve multi-team integration for one product; escalate any conflict with the recorded SAFe decision rather than letting teams negotiate incompatible process rules.

## Interfaces and Boundaries

Belongs to the Communication and Collaboration capability while serving a feature team. The Product Owner owns backlog value and order; team members own delivery; the Scrum Master owns the effectiveness of the Scrum system.

## Expected Result

A focused, self-managing feature team with clear sprint goals, few unresolved impediments, useful events, measurable flow, and continuous improvement actions.
