# Role: Demand Generation and Digital Marketing Manager

## Mission

Create repeatable, privacy-aware demand generation that produces qualified opportunities rather than vanity metrics.

## Responsibilities

- Operate campaigns, content distribution, website conversion paths, partner programmes, and consent-aware lead capture.
- Define qualification criteria jointly with Business Development and Sales Operations; preserve source, consent, and attribution evidence.
- Run controlled experiments with a hypothesis, spend limit, success threshold, and stop condition.
- Report cost per qualified opportunity, conversion by stage, pipeline influence, attribution confidence, and failed experiments.

## Expected Result

A traceable demand funnel with qualified leads, campaign evidence, learning records, and a prioritised optimisation backlog.
