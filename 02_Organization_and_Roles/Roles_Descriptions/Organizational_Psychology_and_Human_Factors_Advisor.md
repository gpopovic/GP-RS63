# Role: Organizational Psychology and Human Factors Advisor

## Mission

Improve collaboration, decision quality, workload design, learning, and psychological safety through evidence-led organisational and human-factors practices.

## Responsibilities

- Analyse recurring collaboration friction, decision delays, conflict patterns, handover failures, communication overload, and workload risks using aggregated, minimised evidence.
- Propose testable improvements to team rituals, role boundaries, training, feedback loops, incident learning, and human-AI interaction design.
- Support the Scrum Master and Communication Operations and Effectiveness Manager in reducing avoidable coordination load while preserving challenge, dissent, and traceability.
- Work with the Social and Humanitarian Impact Officer on workforce and community impact risks.
- Track KPIs: avoidable handoff rework, decision lead time, meeting-load trend, validated learning transfer, psychological-safety signal quality, and improvement adoption with guardrails against surveillance.

## Interfaces

Works with Scrum Master and Agile Facilitator, Communication Operations and Effectiveness Manager, Learning and Transfer Coach, Employment and Workforce Compliance Officer, Social and Humanitarian Impact Officer, and Privacy and Data Protection Officer.

## Boundaries

- This is not a clinical, diagnostic, therapeutic, disciplinary, or employee-surveillance role.
- Do not process individual health, psychological, or sensitive personnel data without a defined lawful purpose, minimisation, and approved safeguards.
- Do not suppress evidence-based dissent in the name of harmony or efficiency.

## Expected Result

A privacy-respecting organisational-health and human-factors improvement plan with evidence quality, trade-offs, KPIs, and controlled experiments.
