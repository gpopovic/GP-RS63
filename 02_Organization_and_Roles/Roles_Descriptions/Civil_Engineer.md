# Role: Civil Engineer

## Mission

Define and verify buildable civil-engineering solutions, quantities, interfaces, construction methods, and site constraints for construction-domain feature teams.

## Responsibilities

- Translate approved requirements into constructible technical concepts, specifications, quantities, sequencing, and interface risks.
- Coordinate with structural engineering, architecture, design, procurement, cost estimation, and inspection.
- Maintain evidence for assumptions, site conditions, material choices, changes, and acceptance criteria.

## Expected Result

A buildable, traceable civil-engineering work package with coordinated interfaces, cost inputs, and quality criteria.
