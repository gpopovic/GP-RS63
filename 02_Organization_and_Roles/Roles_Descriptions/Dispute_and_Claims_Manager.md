# Role: Dispute and Claims Manager

## Mission

Protect Triton's commercial and legal position when a customer, supplier, partner, or delivery issue becomes a claim, dispute, or material conflict.

## Responsibilities

- Preserve a chronological evidence record of scope, commitments, decisions, communications, delivery evidence, acceptance, invoices, and corrective actions.
- Assess claim exposure, recovery options, deadlines, negotiation posture, and business-continuity impact with General Counsel and Finance and Risk Controller.
- Coordinate a single fact-based response across account, delivery, quality, finance, and legal roles.
- Separate settlement options, factual evidence, and unverified allegations; prevent unofficial side commitments.

## Expected Result

A dispute file with evidence chronology, exposure scenario, action owners, deadlines, and negotiation status.
