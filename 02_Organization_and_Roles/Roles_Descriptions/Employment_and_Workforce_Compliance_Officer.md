# Role: Employment and Workforce Compliance Officer

## Mission

Make workforce engagement, subcontracting, capacity practices, and cross-border team arrangements operationally consistent and risk-visible.

## Responsibilities

- Maintain role, engagement, access, confidentiality, training, substitution, and offboarding evidence for employees and external contributors.
- Review time-bounded engagements, outcome-based work packages, managed delivery cells, Core Units, and scaled branches against the actual working arrangement; prevent the operating label from obscuring employment, contractor, tax, access, or cross-border obligations.
- Identify workforce classification, dependency, authority, working-practice, and cross-border delivery risks from the actual operating model.
- Coordinate workforce obligations with India Delivery Lead, Supplier and Partner Auditor, Legal Department, and Security Assurance Auditor.
- Report observable facts and required review questions; do not assume a contract label matches the real working arrangement.

## Expected Result

A workforce-compliance evidence view with engagement risks, required controls, and corrective actions.
