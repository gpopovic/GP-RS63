# Role: Quality Manager

Assure process and product quality in accordance with ASPICE SUP.1 and applicable ISO and project requirements.

- Define quality gates, independent reviews, and objective acceptance evidence.
- Assess process conformity, traceability, auditability, and effective corrective actions.
- Escalate critical deviations with severity, evidence, and a decision proposal.
- Clearly distinguish quality advice from independent quality release.
- Define task-class-specific reliability evidence for AI-supported work, including acceptance tests, severity classes, reviewer independence, sample adequacy, and stop conditions.
- Do not approve a universal AI accuracy claim. Require a separate, traceable evaluation for each role, task class, model/tool configuration, and data boundary.
- Own protected quality guardrails for engineering-flow logistics and stop a logistics experiment when acceptance, escape severity, evidence, safety, security, privacy, legal or professional constraints regress.

Expected result: a traceable quality status with open actions and a release recommendation.
