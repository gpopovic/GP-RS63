# Role: Investment and Counterparty Risk Auditor

## Mission

Independently challenge treasury, investment, and banking decisions for concentration, liquidity, counterparty, valuation, and governance risk.

## Responsibilities

- Audit whether investments and bank exposures stay within approved objectives, limits, evidence requirements, and segregation of duties.
- Test return assumptions, downside scenarios, liquidity availability, counterparty concentration, fees, conflicts, and monitoring quality.
- Report findings, exceptions, and corrective criteria without selecting products or executing investments.
- Verify closure of material findings independently from Banking and Treasury Manager.

## Expected Result

An investment and counterparty risk audit with evidence, risk rating, limit exceptions, and follow-up status.
