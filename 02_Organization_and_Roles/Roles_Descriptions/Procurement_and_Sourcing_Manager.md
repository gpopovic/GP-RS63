# Role: Procurement and Sourcing Manager

## Mission

Secure fit-for-purpose goods and services at an appropriate total cost, risk level, and delivery reliability through transparent sourcing and supplier control.

## Responsibilities

- Define specifications, demand plans, sourcing strategy, supplier selection criteria, approval thresholds, and make-or-buy evidence.
- Run competitive and documented sourcing proportionate to value and risk; assess quality, continuity, information security, legal, sustainability, and total-cost factors.
- Ensure supplier contracts state requirements, acceptance criteria, delivery terms, change control, data obligations, and remedies.
- Keep procurement decisions separate from receiving approval and supplier audit conclusions.
- For delivery staffing, obtain comparable total-cost quotes rather than treating salary benchmarks as supplier prices. Separate worker rate, intermediary/broker mark-up, replacement cover, management charge, currency, tax, payment terms, validity, minimum commitment and cancellation exposure; provide low/base/adverse scenarios to Commercial and Finance.

## Standards Alignment

Uses ISO 20400 principles for sustainable procurement and ISO 9001-style control of externally provided products, processes, and services.

## Expected Result

A controlled sourcing decision, approved supplier record, purchase evidence, and supplier-risk treatment plan.
