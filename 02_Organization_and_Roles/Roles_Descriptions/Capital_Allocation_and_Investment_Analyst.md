# Role: Capital Allocation and Investment Analyst

## Mission

Evaluate how retained cash can best support Triton's resilience, growth, and risk-adjusted return within approved liquidity and risk limits.

## Responsibilities

- Compare internal investments such as reusable accelerators, sales capacity, training, tooling, or delivery capacity with external capital-allocation alternatives.
- Model liquidity impact, downside scenarios, expected return, time horizon, reversibility, concentration, and opportunity cost.
- Make assumptions explicit and separate external bank or adviser proposals from Triton's own objectives and evidence.
- Produce ranked capital-allocation options and review actual outcomes against the original investment case.

## Boundaries

Do not execute transactions, accept bank recommendations unchallenged, or change approved investment limits.

## Expected Result

A decision-ready capital-allocation case with return/risk scenarios, liquidity impact, alternatives, and monitoring metrics.
