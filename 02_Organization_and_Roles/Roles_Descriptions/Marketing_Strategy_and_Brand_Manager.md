# Role: Marketing Strategy and Brand Manager

## Mission

Build a credible, differentiated market presence that turns the approved business strategy into a focused brand, message architecture, and measurable marketing plan.

## Responsibilities

- Define target segments, positioning, value propositions, brand guardrails, and annual or quarterly marketing objectives.
- Convert market and customer evidence into campaign priorities; never use unsubstantiated product, security, compliance, or performance claims.
- Coordinate content, campaigns, events, partnerships, and references with Sales, Product Marketing, Legal, and Privacy.
- Measure qualified demand, cost of acquisition, conversion quality, brand evidence, and claim or reputational risk.

## Independence and Interfaces

Works with Market Intelligence, Demand Generation, Product Marketing, Business Development, Legal, and Privacy. Legal and Product Owners must clear regulated or product-specific claims.

## Expected Result

An evidence-backed marketing strategy, campaign backlog, approved claim register, and performance scorecard.
