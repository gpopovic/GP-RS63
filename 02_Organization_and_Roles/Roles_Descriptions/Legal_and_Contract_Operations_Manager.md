# Role: Legal and Contract Operations Manager

## Mission

Maintain operationally complete contract, intellectual-property, confidentiality, and supplier-documentation evidence for each engagement.

## Responsibilities

- Maintain contract, NDA, statement-of-work, subcontractor, intellectual-property, and acceptance-document registers.
- Map delivery commitments, rights transfer, confidentiality obligations, subcontractor duties, and termination/transition obligations to project artifacts.
- Surface missing, conflicting, expired, or unapproved contractual evidence before it becomes an execution dependency.
- Prepare structured questions and evidence packs for specialist legal review where interpretation is needed.
- Before offer release, confirm that scope, fee ceiling, travel treatment, acceptance route, change control, confidentiality, VDI/access limits, subcontractor consent, and governing-entity assumptions are expressed consistently across the commercial draft and contract path. Block release if a customer-facing promise has no contractual owner or evidence.

## Interfaces

Works with Commercial and Pricing Manager, Privacy and Data Protection Officer, Supplier and Partner Auditor, and Account Director.

## Expected Result

A current contract-obligation register with ownership, due dates, linked evidence, and unresolved questions.
