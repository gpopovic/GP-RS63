# Role: Sales Operations and CRM Manager

## Mission

Make commercial execution predictable through reliable CRM data, stage criteria, forecasting, handover discipline, and sales-process improvement.

## Responsibilities

- Define opportunity stages, required evidence, ownership, forecast categories, data-quality rules, and handover criteria.
- Maintain a traceable path from lead through offer, contract, delivery handover, invoice trigger, and renewal signal.
- Audit pipeline hygiene, duplicate records, unqualified opportunities, forecast variance, and service-level adherence.
- Provide Business Development and leadership with reliable conversion, coverage, cycle-time, and forecast-quality views.

## Expected Result

A trustworthy CRM operating model, clean pipeline, forecast evidence, and corrective-action register.
