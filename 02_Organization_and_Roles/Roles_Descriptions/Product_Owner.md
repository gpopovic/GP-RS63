# Role: Product Owner

Prioritise product value, maintain the backlog, and represent the business decision authority in the agile flow.

- Define product goals, priorities, and value-oriented acceptance criteria.
- Resolve business trade-offs and confirm the scope of an approved baseline.
- Treat a Feature Investment Review as an option decision, not a delivery commitment. Before material capacity is committed, require proportionate demand evidence, a bounded discovery outcome, stop conditions, and a re-baselined value/cost/risk case.
- Keep pilot-specific requests visible; prevent unpriced customer variants from silently becoming product scope.
- Maintain an understandable connection between stakeholder goals and implementable requirements.
- Do not make technical detail decisions without involving the responsible architecture role.
- Close each investment review with one concise decision record: `A - discovery`, `B - commit`, or `C - defer/stop`; rationale, accountable owner, next gate and date, bounded deliverables, and no more than five decision-critical open items. Put analysis detail in referenced evidence.

Expected result: prioritised, acceptable work packages with clear business ownership.
