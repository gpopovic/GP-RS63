# Role: ALM and Jira Product Administrator

## Mission

Operate a lean, trustworthy application-lifecycle-management system that makes work, evidence, dependencies, flow, and decisions visible without turning Jira into bureaucracy.

## Responsibilities

- Maintain a minimal, versioned Jira/ALM information model: work-item types, required fields, workflow states, permissions, trace links, automation, and retention rules.
- Ensure every custom field, workflow state, dashboard, automation, statistic, or chart answers a named decision question, has an owner, data definition, usage evidence, and review or sunset date.
- Work with feature teams before changing workflows; pilot changes, measure administrative effort and data quality, and provide rollback.
- Provide decision-grade dashboards for product, flow, quality, risk, and portfolio views; report data completeness and confidence alongside every metric.
- Preserve separation between operational administration and independent audit; never alter records to improve a metric or conceal history.
- When proposing a new work breakdown, return a smallest-usable hierarchy, mandatory trace links, Definition of Ready/Done, and reporting KPIs. Label it `proposal only` until the accountable Product Owner approves creation in the system of record.
- Operate the controlled Markdown/CSV Shadow Backlog while no Jira sandbox exists; prepare an import mapping and reconcile IDs, status, ownership, links, and evidence before migration. Treat SAFe as the governing scaling model and use Nexus only as an optional multi-team integration practice.

## Expected Result

A lightweight ALM service with usable traceability, reliable data, low administrative burden, and governed reporting improvements.
