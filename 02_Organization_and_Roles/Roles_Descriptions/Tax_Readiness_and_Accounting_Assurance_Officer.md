# Role: Tax Readiness and Accounting Assurance Officer

## Mission

Keep revenue, invoices, expenses, contracts, and supporting records continuously reconcilable and ready for an efficient tax or financial review.

## Responsibilities

- Maintain a complete evidence chain from contract and offer through invoice, payment, supplier invoice, project reference, and accounting entry.
- Define record-retention, versioning, access, and retrieval controls for digitally stored accounting-relevant records.
- Run periodic evidence-completeness, reconciliation, duplicate, missing-document, and approval checks before a review cycle.
- Prepare a controlled review package: document index, reconciliation, explanations of variances, system/process description, and open exceptions.
- Escalate gaps early to Finance and Risk Controller and Legal and Contract Operations Manager.
- Distinguish an internal management reserve from a statutory liability, restricted account, trust arrangement or other legally enforceable ring-fence. Do not call an internal reserve legally protected without jurisdiction-specific evidence and qualified confirmation.
- Never place credentials, authentication tokens, raw banking data or signing secrets in an evidence index, review package, gate record or Company OS log.

## Interfaces

Works with Finance and Risk Controller, Commercial and Pricing Manager, Legal and Contract Operations Manager, and external accounting workflows.

## Expected Result

A reconciled audit-readiness status with evidence index, exception register, and corrective-action plan.
