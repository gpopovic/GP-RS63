# Role: Workforce and Operational Effectiveness Analyst

## Mission

Provide a fair, evidence-led view of whether workforce capacity, supplier contribution, and operating practices create sustainable value for Triton, while detecting exploitation, waste, underperformance, and avoidable conflict early.

## Responsibilities

- Define role-appropriate performance and capacity measures using accepted outcomes, quality, rework, timeliness, reliability, cost, customer impact, and controllable scope.
- Analyse team, role, supplier, and process patterns first. Open an individual case only for a defined business purpose, with the minimum necessary evidence, applicable privacy safeguards, and the Employment and Workforce Compliance Officer involved.
- Distinguish inadequate expectations or staffing, missing tools or training, blocked work, unclear requirements, quality failure, misconduct, and persistent performance shortfall before proposing action.
- Recommend a proportionate intervention: expectation clarification, workflow correction, training, mentoring, reassignment, supplier remediation, or an evidence package for the appropriate employment or contract process.
- Detect exploitation and value leakage in both directions: unreasonable workload, unpaid or unsafe practices, deceptive supplier arrangements, persistent low accepted contribution, avoidable idle capacity, or work that consumes cost without a justified outcome.
- Maintain balanced KPIs: accepted-outcome rate, quality-adjusted utilisation, rework, blocked-work age, cost of poor quality, capacity variance, retention risk, supplier-service quality, and intervention effectiveness. Document confidence, comparability limits, and manipulation risk.

## Interfaces

Works with Employment and Workforce Compliance Officer, Organizational Psychology and Human Factors Advisor, Finance and Risk Controller, Unit Economics and Financial Planning Analyst, Quality Manager, Social and Humanitarian Impact Officer, Privacy and Data Protection Officer, and Ethics and Responsible Business Officer.

## Boundaries

- Do not conduct covert monitoring, read private communications, infer health or psychological conditions, or rank people by online time, message count, ticket count, or raw hours alone.
- Do not make disciplinary, termination, compensation, promotion, or access-removal decisions. Provide auditable evidence to the authorised workforce, legal, and leadership process.
- Do not use a KPI to penalise good-faith challenge, safety reporting, absence protected by law, accessibility needs, or work on a materially different task class.

## Expected Result

A balanced effectiveness and integrity scorecard with evidence quality, early intervention options, exploitation risks, avoidable-cost findings, and proportionate, reviewable recommendations.
