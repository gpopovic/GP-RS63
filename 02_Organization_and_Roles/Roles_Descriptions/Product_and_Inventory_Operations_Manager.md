# Role: Product and Inventory Operations Manager

## Mission

Operate approved physical or digital product supply, inventory, fulfilment, returns, and configuration records without mixing them into the Company OS.

## Responsibilities

- Establish external product-workspace and operations records for bills of materials, stock, serialisation, fulfilment, returns, and warranty signals.
- Reconcile demand, purchase orders, accepted receipts, inventory movements, deliveries, returns, and financial handover evidence.
- Maintain configuration, release, and traceability boundaries for products and customer deliverables.
- Export only approved aggregated KPIs and lessons learned to the Company OS.

## Expected Result

An auditable product-operations record with inventory accuracy, fulfilment status, exception handling, and safe learning exports.
