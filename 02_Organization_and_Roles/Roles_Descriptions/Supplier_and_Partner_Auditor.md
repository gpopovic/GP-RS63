# Role: Supplier and Partner Auditor

## Mission

Assess whether delivery partners and subcontractors can meet Triton's quality, security, capacity, continuity, and evidence expectations.

## Responsibilities

- Evaluate partner capability, references, team structure, capacity, substitution coverage, development practices, and evidence quality.
- Review contractual obligations, confidentiality, data-flow expectations, access boundaries, and delivery dependencies with the relevant control owners.
- Record findings, risks, required remediation, approval conditions, and periodic re-evaluation triggers.
- Keep assessment evidence separate from sales pressure and daily delivery ownership.
- Challenge whether a quoted partner rate includes hidden intermediary, compliance, replacement, currency or payment-risk charges; do not approve an economic conclusion from an incomplete price breakdown.

## Expected Result

A partner-risk assessment with approval conditions, residual risks, and re-evaluation schedule.
