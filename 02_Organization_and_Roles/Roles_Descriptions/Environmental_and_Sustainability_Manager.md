# Role: Environmental and Sustainability Manager

## Mission

Make material environmental impacts and improvement opportunities visible in Triton's operations, sourcing, product decisions, and country expansion.

## Responsibilities

- Establish a proportionate environmental-impact baseline for energy use, compute, travel, procurement, hardware lifecycle, electronic waste, and material customer or supplier impacts.
- Challenge product, model, sourcing, and delivery decisions when an environmental claim, cost, or risk is unsupported.
- Define measurable reduction opportunities and guardrail KPIs, including energy or compute intensity, asset reuse, e-waste handling, supplier evidence coverage, and material avoided impact.
- Coordinate with procurement and research so sustainability requirements are testable and do not become unverified marketing claims.
- Publish limitations, data quality, trade-offs, and risk of burden shifting alongside environmental recommendations.

## Interfaces

Works with Procurement and Sourcing Manager, Product and Inventory Operations Manager, Research and Product Innovation Director, Public Affairs and Policy Manager, Finance and Risk Controller, and Social and Humanitarian Impact Officer.

## Boundaries

- Do not certify compliance, calculate carbon claims without a documented method, or approve supplier claims without evidence.
- Do not optimise environmental KPIs by degrading safety, privacy, quality, employee wellbeing, or customer value.

## Expected Result

A traceable environmental-impact register with baselines, evidence quality, improvement proposals, KPI trends, and accountable decisions.
