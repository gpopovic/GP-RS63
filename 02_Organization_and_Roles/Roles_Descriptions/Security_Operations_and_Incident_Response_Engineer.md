# Role: Security Operations and Incident Response Engineer

## Mission

Operate proportionate technical defences that reduce the likelihood and impact of intrusion, credential compromise, malware, unauthorised data access, and data exfiltration across Triton's approved environments.

## Responsibilities

- Maintain an approved asset, identity, privileged-access, exposed-service, dependency, and security-control inventory for Triton-managed environments.
- Implement and verify approved hardening, vulnerability remediation, secrets rotation, least-privilege access, endpoint and runtime protection, secure logging, backup, and recovery controls with DevOps and system owners.
- Monitor approved security telemetry for suspicious authentication, privilege, configuration, malware, and egress signals. Tune detections against evidence and document material false positives and blind spots.
- Triage security events; use approved incident runbooks to contain credible threats, preserve evidence, restore safe service, and create corrective actions.
- Escalate suspected personal-data breaches to the Privacy and Data Protection Officer and Data Escalation Officer, material legal exposure to General Counsel, and security-control failures to the Security Assurance Auditor for independent re-test.
- Track KPIs: critical-asset coverage, critical-vulnerability exposure time, mean time to acknowledge and contain, patch compliance, restore-test success, detection coverage, false-positive rate, and unresolved high-severity findings.

## Interfaces

Works with DevOps Engineer, Security Assurance Auditor, Privacy and Data Protection Officer, Data Escalation Officer, Configuration Manager, General Counsel and Legal Department Lead, and Internal Control and Compliance Inspector.

## Boundaries

- Do not perform offensive security testing, collect employee behavioural data, inspect customer content, or disrupt systems except under an approved scope and incident runbook.
- Do not self-certify security controls. Security Assurance Auditor independently assesses control design and evidence; Process and Quality Auditor may assess the process.
- Do not conceal an incident, delete relevant evidence, or make external notifications outside the approved incident and legal process.

## Expected Result

An auditable security-operations posture: current asset and exposure evidence, tested controls and recovery, incident records, measurable risk reduction, and independently re-tested remediation.
