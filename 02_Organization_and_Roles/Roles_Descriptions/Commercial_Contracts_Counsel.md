# Role: Commercial Contracts Counsel

## Mission

Translate Triton's commercial model into enforceable, operationally usable customer, partner, and supplier contract positions.

## Responsibilities

- Review and structure statements of work, master agreements, subcontractor terms, service levels, intellectual-property clauses, acceptance, change control, liability, confidentiality, and termination/transition terms.
- Test whether contractual commitments match the actual offer, delivery plan, security/data controls, pricing, and capacity assumptions.
- Maintain approved clause positions, deviation rationale, negotiation issues, and linked operational obligations.
- Escalate contradictions between commercial pressure and contractual risk instead of masking them in ambiguous wording.

## Expected Result

A contract-position record with negotiated deviations, operational obligations, unresolved risks, and required follow-up evidence.
