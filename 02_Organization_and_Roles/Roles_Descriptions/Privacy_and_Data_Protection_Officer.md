# Role: Privacy and Data Protection Officer

## Mission

Make data processing, cross-border access, and data minimisation requirements explicit before work packages or tools receive data.

Maintain the independent advisory and monitoring function required of Triton's designated Data Protection Officer where a designation is required or chosen.

## Responsibilities

- Classify project data and define allowed storage, access, transfer, retention, and test-data patterns.
- For local deployments, distinguish customer-local processing from the external product workspace and the Company OS. Define whether production data may be processed locally and explicitly prohibit unapproved transfer into Company OS, AI chats, development/test fixtures, telemetry, or external providers.
- Review processor/subprocessor and third-country transfer dependencies against the approved project data-flow record.
- Require a traceable basis for pseudonymisation, synthetic-data substitution, access restrictions, and incident escalation.
- Provide operational data-protection controls and questions for specialist review rather than unsupported legal conclusions.
- Advise on, and monitor the performance of, data-protection impact assessments when they are required or proportionate to the risk.
- Monitor data-protection controls, awareness, and audit findings; report material unresolved privacy risk directly to the highest appropriate management level.
- Remain free from instructions on the exercise of DPO tasks and declare any conflict of interest. Do not own commercial data-processing targets, system delivery, or security operations that this role must independently assess.
- Act as the privacy contact point for supervisory-authority or data-subject matters through the approved legal process.

## Interfaces

Works with General Counsel and Legal Department Lead, Legal and Contract Operations Manager, Security Assurance Auditor, Data Escalation Officer, India Delivery Lead, and Organizational Psychology and Human Factors Advisor.

## Expected Result

A project data-flow and control register with approved data categories, allowed processing locations, and open remediation actions.
