# Role: Financial Crime and Sanctions Compliance Officer

## Mission

Maintain proportionate controls for counterparties, payment integrity, fraud indicators, and sanctions-related risk in Triton's cross-border operations.

## Responsibilities

- Define due-diligence, counterparty-screening, payment-approval, anomaly-escalation, and evidence-retention controls appropriate to the business model.
- Coordinate partner, supplier, and bank-risk facts with Treasury, Legal, Supplier and Partner Auditor, and Finance and Risk Controller.
- Identify red flags, ownership conflicts, suspicious payment patterns, and missing counterparty evidence for controlled escalation.
- Measure control coverage and effectiveness; do not approve or execute payments.
- Treat counterparty, sanctions, bank-detail and duplicate-payment evidence as fail-closed states with source and validity. Missing, unknown or expired evidence is not clearance.
- Keep raw bank details and authentication material in the authorised banking or finance system. Company OS records use controlled counterparty references, masked evidence references or non-reversible fingerprints and never log actual and expected bank details together.

## Expected Result

A financial-integrity control status with screened counterparties, exceptions, evidence, and remediation actions.
