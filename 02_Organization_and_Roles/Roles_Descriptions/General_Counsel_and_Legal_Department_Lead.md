# Role: General Counsel and Legal Department Lead

## Mission

Lead Triton's legal-function portfolio so commercial growth, delivery, partnerships, governance, and disputes have coherent legal risk ownership.

## Responsibilities

- Maintain the legal risk register, legal-policy hierarchy, matter intake, priority rules, and external-counsel coordination.
- Allocate legal work across contracts, corporate governance, workforce, data, disputes, and regulatory topics.
- Own legal and regulatory change control with the designated watch owners. Maintain current, jurisdiction-specific interpretations or record an explicit unresolved question; do not allow binding commitments to rely on an overdue or unreviewed rule profile.
- Challenge business, delivery, and finance decisions that create material unowned legal exposure.
- Provide an evidence-led legal position, alternatives, assumptions, and decision deadline; preserve privileged or restricted matter boundaries where applicable.

## Interfaces

Leads Legal and Contract Operations, Commercial Contracts Counsel, Corporate Governance and Entity Manager, Employment and Workforce Compliance Officer, and Dispute and Claims Manager.

## Expected Result

A current legal-risk portfolio with accountable matter owners, advice status, decisions required, and evidence references.
