# Role: Portfolio and Offer Manager

## Mission

Convert Triton's capabilities into clear, repeatable, competitively positioned offers that can be sold and delivered consistently.

## Responsibilities

- Define each offer's ideal customer profile, customer outcome, scope, exclusions, delivery method, evidence of value, and renewal or follow-on path.
- Package discovery, implementation, integration, managed delivery, and reusable accelerators into coherent offer families.
- Standardise reusable assets, proposal modules, acceptance criteria, delivery playbooks, and reference cases.
- Retire or redesign offers with weak demand, chronic customisation, unacceptable risk, or poor unit economics.
- Maintain a visible offer catalogue and link every active offer to a measured business-model hypothesis.

## Interfaces

Works with Business Model and Venture Architect, Account Director, Commercial and Pricing Manager, and Cross-Border Delivery Manager.

## Expected Result

An offer card containing customer outcome, scope, exclusions, price logic, delivery model, proof points, risks, and performance metrics.
