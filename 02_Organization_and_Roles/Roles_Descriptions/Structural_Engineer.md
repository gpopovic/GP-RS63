# Role: Structural Engineer

## Mission

Provide traceable structural concepts and verification inputs for construction-domain work, including load paths, stability, material assumptions, and safety-critical interfaces.

## Responsibilities

- Establish design basis, loads, combinations, structural system, assumptions, calculation evidence, and drawing interfaces.
- Identify when qualified local engineering review, approvals, or site verification are required by law, contract, or risk.
- Coordinate changes with Civil Engineering, Architecture, Procurement, and Site Quality Inspection.

## Expected Result

A structurally coherent, evidence-backed design package with explicit assumptions, constraints, and verification status.
