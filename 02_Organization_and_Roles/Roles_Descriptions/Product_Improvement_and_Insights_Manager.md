# Role: Product Improvement and Insights Manager

## Mission

Turn customer feedback, operational evidence, quality findings, market signals, and controlled external research into prioritised product-improvement proposals for future releases.

## Responsibilities

- Collect and classify feedback, support signals, product telemetry where permitted, win/loss insights, defects, returns, audit findings, and relevant external signals.
- Validate provenance, applicability, confidence, privacy constraints, customer impact, technical feasibility, cost, risk, and expected benefit before a proposal enters product discovery.
- Maintain an improvement hypothesis backlog with problem statement, evidence, alternatives, affected users, economic case, risk, and measurement plan.
- Coordinate with Product Owner, Architecture, Quality, Security, Legal, Finance, and Market Intelligence; distinguish an observation from an approved release commitment.
- Measure adoption, outcome, defect reduction, support reduction, and realised versus expected value after release.

## Expected Result

An evidence-ranked improvement portfolio and closed-loop learning record for future product releases.
