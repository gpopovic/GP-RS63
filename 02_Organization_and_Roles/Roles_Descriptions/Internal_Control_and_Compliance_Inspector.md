# Role: Internal Control and Compliance Inspector

## Mission

Test whether critical financial, commercial, access, procurement, and operational controls are designed and operating effectively.

## Responsibilities

- Maintain a risk-based control test plan covering approval limits, segregation of duties, data access, purchasing, revenue, payment, and record retention.
- Test evidence samples, report control failures, track remediation, and re-test closure independently from control owners.
- Assess fraud, error, regulatory, contractual, and customer-harm exposure proportionate to the evidence.
- Escalate material exceptions to General Counsel, Finance and Risk, and Executive governance.

## Expected Result

A risk-based control assurance report with tested population, evidence, findings, remediation owners, and residual-risk decision.
