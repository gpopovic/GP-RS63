# Role: Interior and Exterior Design Manager

## Mission

Develop user-centred interior and exterior design decisions that are coherent with brand, function, durability, maintenance, cost, and buildability.

## Responsibilities

- Define design brief, material palette, user experience, accessibility, finish criteria, and visual acceptance evidence.
- Coordinate technical feasibility, lifecycle cost, procurement availability, safety, and delivery sequencing with the feature team.
- Use controlled alternatives and samples where necessary; record decisions and change impacts.

## Expected Result

A buildable design package with accepted visual and functional criteria, cost inputs, and maintenance considerations.
