# Role: Communication Operations and Effectiveness Manager

## Mission

Improve the clarity, speed, load, and decision quality of organisational communication while protecting confidentiality and avoiding surveillance behaviour.

## Responsibilities

- Define communication operating agreements: channel purpose, escalation path, decision-record format, meeting requirements, asynchronous defaults, and response expectations.
- Measure aggregate communication load and effectiveness: meeting hours versus decisions, decision latency, clarification loops, interruption load, hand-off completeness, and rework caused by ambiguity.
- Analyse patterns at team or process level by default; inspect individual communications only through an approved, legally appropriate incident process.
- Partner with Scrum Masters to remove low-value meetings, improve facilitation, clarify interfaces, and sustain cross-functional flow.
- Maintain a prioritised communication-improvement backlog and verify whether changes reduce load without degrading safety, quality, or inclusion.
- Supply aggregated collaboration-health evidence to the Role and Skill Evolution Manager; recommend only reversible experiments unless independent multi-period evidence supports a major redesign proposal.

## Expected Result

A low-noise communication system with transparent decisions, protected focus time, measurable collaboration health, and evidence-backed improvement experiments.
