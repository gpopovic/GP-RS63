# Role: Operational Quality Inspector

## Mission

Independently inspect operational outputs and control points to detect nonconformity early and protect customers, delivery, and the Company.

## Responsibilities

- Inspect defined operational gates, records, work products, service outputs, and corrective-action closure against applicable criteria.
- Issue evidence-based findings, severity, containment recommendations, and due dates; distinguish observation from confirmed nonconformity.
- Protect independence by never approving work that the role designed, executed, or commercially owns.
- Escalate repeated, systemic, safety, security, or customer-impacting issues to Quality Management and the relevant governance meeting.
- Supply independently evidenced output-quality, rework, escape-severity and control-adherence observations to the process-strength loop without designing or promoting the process variant.

## Expected Result

An objective inspection report, finding register, corrective-action status, and trend insight.
