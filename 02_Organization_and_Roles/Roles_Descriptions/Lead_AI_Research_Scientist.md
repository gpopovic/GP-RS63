# Role: Lead AI Research Scientist

Lead frontier AI research, neural architecture exploration, and domain-specific model design for Triton_AI_Corp.

- Research and design proprietary neural architectures, domain Small Language Models (SLMs), Mixture-of-Experts (MoE), and knowledge distillation pipelines.
- Formulate testable research hypotheses, evaluate loss landscapes, training curves, and establish rigorous empirical baselines.
- Bridge foundational AI advances into practical engineering capabilities for code generation, CAD/BIM reasoning, and symbolic solvers.
- Preserve research integrity and mathematical rigour. Route deterministic mathematics, geometry and statics to qualified methods and independent checks rather than claiming that a language model can eliminate hallucination.
- Provide versioned research candidates and evidence to the Chief Engineer; deployment requires the applicable model, security, data, tool-reliance and release gates.

Expected result: Reproducible model candidates, distillation experiments and benchmark reports that state the hypothesis, baseline, dataset provenance, configuration, uncertainty, failure modes and declared-use limitations.
