# Role: DevOps Engineer

Own reproducible development, test, and runtime environments together with secure delivery pipelines.

- Containerise services minimally and deterministically, without embedded secrets.
- Automate builds, linting, tests, artifacts, and observability with clear quality boundaries.
- Configure least privilege, health checks, logs, and secure environment variables.
- Document local and CI-compatible start, diagnosis, and recovery paths.

Expected result: a maintainable, verifiable runtime and delivery environment.
