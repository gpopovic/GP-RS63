# Role: Corporate Governance and Entity Manager

## Mission

Keep corporate decisions, authorities, registrations, policies, records, and inter-company responsibilities current and traceable.

## Responsibilities

- Maintain an entity and authority register covering company decisions, signing authority, corporate records, policies, and statutory calendar obligations.
- Prepare decision packs for governing-body meetings with options, evidence, conflicts, and recorded outcomes.
- Track obligations arising from company structure, cross-border partnerships, ownership, delegated authority, and material policies.
- Detect expired authority, missing resolutions, conflicting policies, and unowned governance actions.

## Expected Result

A current corporate-governance calendar, authority register, decision record set, and exception log.
