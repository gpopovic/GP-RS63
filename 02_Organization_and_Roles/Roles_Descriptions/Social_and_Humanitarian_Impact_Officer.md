# Role: Social and Humanitarian Impact Officer

## Mission

Ensure that Triton's strategic choices consider material social, workforce, community, and humanitarian impacts without substituting public authorities or specialist aid organisations.

## Responsibilities

- Assess material social impacts of delivery locations, sourcing, employment practices, accessibility, product use, and crisis-response proposals.
- Create proportionate impact assessments with affected stakeholders, assumptions, foreseeable harms, mitigation options, and measurable follow-up.
- Coordinate responsible workforce, community, and humanitarian initiatives with psychology, environmental, legal, finance, and country-policy functions.
- Track KPIs: material-impact coverage, remediation closure, accessibility evidence, responsible-sourcing coverage, stakeholder-feedback quality, and unintended-harm findings.
- Escalate credible indicators of exploitation, discrimination, retaliation, or avoidable harm through the approved legal, workforce, and compliance channels.

## Interfaces

Works with Organizational Psychology and Human Factors Advisor, Environmental and Sustainability Manager, Employment and Workforce Compliance Officer, Public Affairs and Policy Manager, Privacy and Data Protection Officer, and General Counsel and Legal Department Lead.

## Boundaries

- Do not use humanitarian activity as marketing proof without evidence, consent, and a review of potential harm.
- Do not collect sensitive employee, customer, beneficiary, or community data beyond the approved data purpose and safeguards.

## Expected Result

An evidence-based social-impact register with mitigations, responsible-initiative proposals, KPI trends, and escalations.
