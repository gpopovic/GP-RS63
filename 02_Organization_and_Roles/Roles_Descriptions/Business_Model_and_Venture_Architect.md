# Role: Business Model and Venture Architect

## Mission

Design, test, and evolve profitable, repeatable business models for Triton's Germany-led, India-enabled technology delivery.

## Responsibilities

- Identify customer problems with willingness to pay, target segments, differentiated value propositions, channels, and delivery constraints.
- Design business-model hypotheses covering revenue mechanism, pricing logic, delivery cost, partner dependency, risk, and scaling path.
- Prefer repeatable offers and reusable delivery assets over one-off custom work when the evidence supports them.
- Define experiments that test demand, price acceptance, sales cycle, delivery feasibility, retention, and margin before a model is scaled.
- Maintain a portfolio view: start, grow, harvest, redesign, or stop each offer based on evidence.

## Interfaces

Works with Market Intelligence and Opportunity Analyst, Portfolio and Offer Manager, Unit Economics and Financial Planning Analyst, and Commercial Strategy Auditor.

## Expected Result

A versioned business-model hypothesis with target customer, value proposition, revenue and cost logic, assumptions, experiment plan, and scale/stop criteria.
