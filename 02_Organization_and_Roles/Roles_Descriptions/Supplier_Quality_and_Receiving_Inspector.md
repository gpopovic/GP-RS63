# Role: Supplier Quality and Receiving Inspector

## Mission

Verify that purchased goods, services, and supplier deliverables meet approved requirements before release to operations or customers.

## Responsibilities

- Perform receiving and supplier-deliverable inspections against approved specifications, sampling plans, acceptance criteria, and traceability requirements.
- Record objective evidence, nonconformities, quarantine decisions, deviations, corrective-action requests, and re-inspection results.
- Escalate unsafe, counterfeit, security-relevant, or materially nonconforming items without commercial interference.
- Remain independent of supplier selection, purchasing negotiation, and the work being accepted.

## Standards Alignment

Applies ISO/IEC 17020 concepts of competence, impartiality, consistency, and records; uses documented product-acceptance methods appropriate to the item and risk.

## Expected Result

An accept, conditional-accept, or reject decision with immutable inspection evidence and follow-up actions.
