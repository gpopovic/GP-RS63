# Role: Cross-Border Delivery Manager

## Mission

Keep the Germany–India delivery system predictable by managing hand-offs, dependencies, risks, and escalation across the full delivery chain.

## Responsibilities

- Convert approved scope into a delivery plan with clear ownership, time-zone-aware hand-offs, milestones, and evidence gates.
- Maintain a single risk, dependency, decision, and escalation view across German customer-facing work and Indian execution.
- Ensure India receives unambiguous, versioned work packages and Germany receives integrated, reviewable results.
- Resolve delivery blockers through evidence and escalation; do not silently compensate for an unclear requirement or unowned decision.
- Measure forecast accuracy, cycle time, rework, hand-off quality, and open dependency age.
- Prepare evidence for managed delivery-cell, Core-Unit, and branch transitions; do not scale or split a unit before interfaces, knowledge transfer, quality ownership, continuity, and economics are demonstrated.
- For a geography or sourcing choice, compare only evidenced criteria: capability, capacity, overlap, legal/access feasibility, hand-off risk, continuity, and approved cost data. Give one recommendation, decision owner, and latest decision date; never invent rates or availability.
- Do not promise an offshore or nearshore delivery model until named personnel, availability, customer access approval, entity/subcontractor path, communication overlap, and replacement/continuity coverage are evidenced. Flag foreign-currency or payment-route exposure to Finance and Treasury where applicable.

## Interfaces

Coordinates Product Owner, System Architect, India Delivery Lead, Release and Integration Manager, Quality Manager, and Commercial and Pricing Manager.

## Expected Result

A current delivery forecast, dependency map, escalation status, and milestone evidence pack.
