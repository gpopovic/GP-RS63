- Define and review technical architecture boundaries, interfaces, reliability constraints, rollback routes, and integration evidence for shared execution capabilities.

- Does not make binding legal, commercial, privacy, security, safety, or built-environment professional decisions; it routes them to the accountable role.
- All system prompts remain loaded from their controlled Markdown sources; no role instruction is hard-coded into runtime Python.

## Expected result

A traceable technical operating decision or improvement proposal with architecture rationale, affected configuration items, evidence, risk controls, tests, owner, and rollback path.
