# Collaboration Health and Evolution Policy

## Purpose

Measure whether role interactions create reliable flow, constructive challenge, sustainable workload, and accepted outcomes. A role profile is healthy only when its interfaces do not move hidden cost, errors, overload, or conflict to another role.

## Collaboration Scorecard

| Metric | Calculation | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Interface acceptance rate | handoffs accepted without material clarification or rework / reviewed handoffs | Segment by interface and task class. |
| Clarification-loop rate | material clarification cycles / reviewed handoffs | A justified safety or legal question is not waste. |
| Coordination rework ratio | interface-caused correction effort / total work-package effort | Evidence identifies the interface, not a person to blame. |
| Decision lead time | time from ready evidence to accountable decision record | Pair with decision reversal and quality outcome. |
| Constructive dissent resolution | dissent resolved or escalated with evidence / material dissent | Do not target zero dissent. |
| Cross-role defect escape rate | defects found after an interface or gate / accepted cross-role outputs | Critical escape triggers containment, not experimentation. |
| Sustainable capacity signal | committed effort / verified available capacity, plus lawful voluntary anonymous pulse | Never infer health from messages, voice, online time, or private content. |
| Meeting decision yield | meetings with decision, owner, due date, evidence link / reviewed decision meetings | Attendance and meeting hours alone are not performance metrics. |

## Evolution Rules

1. Observe comparable interface evidence without changing the baseline.
2. After three comparable observations of one reversible issue, propose one micro-trial: clarify a handoff, tighten an input/output contract, change an agenda, add/remove a step, or add a deputy route.
3. Compare the micro-trial with the baseline. It must improve the named problem without degrading acceptance, defects, quality, safety, privacy, cost, capacity, or customer outcome.
4. A role split, merge, mandate change, or major process redesign requires three independent signals across at least two categories (quality, flow, workload, cost, risk), repeated across three comparable periods, plus independent audit or review.
5. A severe failure requires containment and strict mode; it is never evidence for an automatic redesign.
6. The Evolution Manager can recommend a trial only. The configuration owner makes the recorded human decision and preserves rollback.

## Owners

Communication Operations owns communication-flow data. Scrum Masters own team-event effectiveness. Organizational Psychology and Human Factors Advisor assesses human-factors interpretation. Quality Manager and Process and Quality Auditor provide independent quality evidence. Continuous Improvement Architect proposes small experiments. Role and Skill Evolution Manager coordinates candidate profile changes.
