# Creative Option Exploration Policy

## Purpose

Preserve human intuition and broad option discovery while keeping requirements, hard constraints, evidence and accountable decisions explicit. This policy applies when a task benefits from divergent concepts followed by multi-objective comparison. It does not make sketching, a named aesthetic, a daily ritual or AI generation mandatory for every task.

## Controlled Flow

1. Record the decision question, declared use, accountable human decision owner and any active product-workspace boundary.
2. Capture an optional human anchor such as a sketch, narrative, mood board, prototype or counterexample. Record its reference without treating it as a requirement by default.
3. Challenge the brief with explicit alternative hypotheses and validation questions. Never infer a person's unconscious need, cultural preference or consent from keywords.
4. Generate a bounded set of meaningfully different options. Record provenance, assumptions, licences, model/tool route and cost cap where AI or an external tool contributes.
5. Apply hard safety, legal, contractual, privacy, security, professional and approved project constraints before comparison. Missing or failed required constraints make an option inadmissible.
6. Compare admissible options across declared objectives with units, direction and source identity. Keep qualitative criteria as explicit human ratings with their method and uncertainty; do not disguise them as objective measurements.
7. A Pareto front contains all and only non-dominated admissible options for the declared objective vector. A weighted score or top-N ranking is not a Pareto front.
8. Present trade-offs, sensitivity, missing evidence and rejected options. The engine returns decision support and never an automatic winning design.
9. The named human owner selects, requests evidence, iterates or stops. Product-specific approval and professional responsibility remain in the external product workspace.

## Execution Modes and Hardening

- `RULE_BOUND` is used when the work is defined by an exact schema, syntax, interface, calculation contract or other deterministic rule set. The declared validator controls acceptance. Reproducibility is claimed only for the observed configuration, and a zero-tolerance rule applies only to the named contract violation rather than to all defects.
- `EXPLORATORY` is used for option discovery, architecture, refactoring hypotheses and innovation. It requires a bounded technique set, source references, an option budget and the controlled flow above. SCAMPER, reframing and other named techniques are candidates, not universal requirements; unnamed or unsourced heuristic lists are not silently invented.

Before an exploratory option can become an implementation candidate, evaluate the declared need, feasibility, viability, hard obligations, audit/security implications, operability, maintainability and rollback. These are evidence questions, not four ceremonial gates required for every trivial task.

Quality observations may recommend reducing exploration breadth or returning to a controlled template. Thresholds belong to the task-class quality contract and record source, measurement window and owner. No defect, rework or rejection metric automatically changes the active route; critical escapes enter containment, while normal signals produce a proposal for the named human decision owner.

## Creative Patterns

Thinking on paper, briefing inversion, Kansei methods, shape grammars, Wabi-Sabi, sightline studies, funnel exploration and analogous methods may be offered as optional lenses. Their use depends on the brief, people involved and declared decision. No aesthetic lens becomes a universal gate, bonus or presumed customer preference.

## Evidence and Status

Record the anchor reference, hypotheses, option identities, objective definitions, source references, hard-constraint evidence, admissibility decisions, non-dominance result, uncertainty and human disposition. A successful calculation is `CHECK_PASSED` for the supplied data; it does not establish design quality, compliance, construction release or customer acceptance.

## Roles and Boundaries

- Product Owner frames the decision and value question.
- Requirements Engineer preserves stated needs, assumptions and validation questions.
- The relevant design or technical role owns option feasibility.
- Safety, legal, security, privacy and professional roles determine their applicable hard constraints.
- Configuration Manager protects option and source identity.
- The named human authority owns the selection or stop decision.

No agent may infer approval, waive a hard constraint, select a binding design or move product evidence into the Company OS.
