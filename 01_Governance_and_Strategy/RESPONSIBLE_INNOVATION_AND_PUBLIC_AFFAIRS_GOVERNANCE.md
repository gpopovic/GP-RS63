# Responsible Innovation, Public Affairs, and Impact Governance

## Purpose

This configuration item governs how Triton turns external best practices, research signals, policy developments, and impact concerns into controlled improvements. It prevents a useful idea from silently becoming a product commitment, legal position, political activity, or unverified sustainability claim.

## Controlled Intake

1. Capture the source, owner, date, jurisdiction, licence or reuse basis, claim, and intended use in an intake record.
2. Classify it as an observation, candidate practice, research hypothesis, regulatory question, or policy signal.
3. Assign the relevant roles: Research and Product Innovation Director for research; Public Affairs and Policy Manager for policy engagement; Ethics and Responsible Business Officer for material ethical trade-offs; Environmental and Sustainability Manager for environmental impact; Social and Humanitarian Impact Officer and Organizational Psychology and Human Factors Advisor for social and human impacts.
4. Request Legal, Privacy, Security, Quality, Finance, or Product review where the claim affects their control boundary.
5. Run a bounded experiment or analysis with success criteria, stop criteria, data boundary, cost limit, and decision owner.
6. Record a promote, trial, keep, rollback, retire, or escalate decision. Canonical roles, skills, and policies change only through configuration management.

## Public-Affairs Boundary

Triton may take transparent, lawful positions and engage with public consultations, standards bodies, industry associations, and legitimate stakeholders. Every engagement must have a defined purpose, jurisdiction check, legal review where needed, conflict assessment, approval record, and retained material. Triton prohibits bribery, covert influence, disinformation, facilitation payments, prohibited political finance, and undisclosed lobbying activity.

Public Affairs, Legal, Corporate Governance, Finance, and Ethics actively search for lawful strategic advantage through funding, sandboxes, standards work, compliant structures, procurement routes, and legitimate location or timing choices. A proposal based on legal ambiguity must state its legal basis, the rule's protective purpose, affected stakeholders, commercial benefit, alternative, and residual risk. It may not rely on sham arrangements, concealment, deception, rights evasion, or transfer of hidden harm.

## Research and Product Boundary

Research work is exploratory. It may create evidence and pilot proposals; it cannot itself promise a feature, accept customer data, approve a release, or commit company funds beyond an approved experiment. Product and investment gates retain their existing authority.

## Innovation Maturity Path

Every AI-enabled opportunity follows a proportionate, evidence-based path. A stage may be skipped only when its decision evidence already exists and the accountable gate records why.

| Stage | Objective | Minimum evidence | Exit decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Potential analysis | Establish a material customer or operating problem, expected value, constraints, and alternatives. | Problem statement, hypothesis, feasibility and risk scan, cost/time box, and stop criteria. | Stop, research, or admit a bounded prototype. |
| Lab small | Test the technical concept using synthetic or otherwise approved restricted data. | Reproducible prototype, evaluation method, data-flow record, and failure findings. | Stop, refine, or expand the experiment. |
| Lab full | Evaluate the candidate against representative conditions without weakening approved data boundaries. | Benchmark results, cost/latency evidence, security/privacy review, and acceptance thresholds. | Stop, revise, or propose a controlled field study. |
| Field study | Validate usability and operational value in the explicitly approved operational environment. | Approved scope, named owner, monitoring plan, rollback plan, user feedback, and incident route. | Stop, correct, or request product/release investment. |
| Roll-out | Operate a proven capability as a maintained product or internal service. | Applicable product, security, privacy, quality, commercial, support, and configuration evidence. | Release, monitor, improve, or retire. |

Production or historical customer data is never an implied input to a stage. Its use requires the project data-flow record, the approved processing location, and the applicable customer, privacy, security, and legal approvals. Engineering fixtures and Company OS learning records remain synthetic or aggregated as required by the project workspace contract.

## Human, Social, and Environmental Boundary

Environmental and social recommendations state their method, assumptions, data quality, trade-offs, and potential unintended effects. Psychological and human-factors work uses aggregated, minimised evidence and is never clinical assessment or employee surveillance. Sensitive data follows the project data-flow record and privacy controls.

## Core KPI Guardrails

- Research: experiment cycle time, evidence quality, validated opportunity rate, transfer rate, and cost of learning.
- Public affairs: country-policy coverage, early-risk detection, transparent-engagement coverage, and unresolved compliance findings.
- Environment: baseline coverage, compute or energy intensity where measurable, asset reuse, e-waste evidence, and avoided material impact.
- Social and human factors: mitigation closure, accessibility evidence, coordination rework, meeting-load trend, learning transfer, and unintended-harm findings.

No activity count, sentiment score, or cost metric may be used alone to judge people, teams, or societal impact.
