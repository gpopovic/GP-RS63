# Enterprise Risk Transfer and Insurance Control

## Purpose

Connect material enterprise and project risks to prevention, contractual allocation, contingency, insurance investigation, and retained-risk decisions without claiming coverage or legal protection that has not been verified.

## Controlled Risk-Transfer Record

For each material exposure record the risk ID, scenario, cause, consequence, affected entity and project, jurisdiction, owner, inherent likelihood and impact basis, preventive and detective controls, contractual allocation, candidate insurance class, requested limits and deductible, exclusions and conditions, broker or insurer evidence, policy number and period when active, residual risk, decision, review trigger, and evidence location.

Possible insurance classes include cyber, professional liability, general liability, property, construction or erection risk, business interruption, directors and officers, crime, and other jurisdiction-specific products. The class name does not establish scope. Only the executed current policy wording, endorsements, insured parties, territory, limits, deductibles, exclusions, notification conditions, and qualified advice establish the evidenced coverage position.

## Decision Rules

- Prefer prevention and controlled delivery; insurance does not prove due care, compliance, fitness, or absence of negligence.
- A company form does not guarantee complete separation of personal and corporate liability. Legal counsel determines the applicable entity, conduct, guarantee, capital, tax, employment, and jurisdictional effects.
- A hash or test result identifies evidence or proves a named technical property; it is not a notarisation, legal defence, state-of-the-art proof, or insurance condition unless an authorised source expressly says so.
- Never label a risk `CONTROLLED`, `MITIGATED`, or `LIVE` without current control evidence and an owner-approved residual-risk decision. Missing policy wording is `NOT_ASSESSED`, not insured.
- Project-specific site, contractor, structural, permit, payment, and safety risks remain in the external project workspace. Triton may hold only the approved aggregate or control reference.

## Review and Authority

Finance and Risk Controller maintains the corporate record. General Counsel validates legal allocation; Privacy and Security review their exposures; Procurement verifies supplier evidence; the relevant project owner maintains project risk; an insurance professional validates proposed coverage. Only the authorised human may buy, renew, cancel, notify, settle, or accept residual risk.
