# Triton Organisation Operating Model

## Single Source of Structure

`ROLE_REGISTRY.json` is the machine-readable index of every canonical role, core assistant, and internal coach. Each member maps to a Markdown instruction under `02_Organization_and_Roles/`. The role-registry validator must pass before a role is considered part of the organisational model.

`COMPANY_CONFIGURATION_INDEX.json` is the machine-readable index of all Company OS configuration items. `COMPANY_CONFIGURATION_MANAGEMENT.md` governs their controlled lifecycle; `ROLE_ENGAGEMENT_MODEL.md` governs how one role may serve as Actor, Advisor, Validator, Auditor, Panel member, or Chair without compromising independence.

## Shared Behaviour

The Collaboration Constitution is loaded with every canonical role instruction. It gives every role the same operating rights and duties: act within defined authority, support the system objective, challenge a specific weak claim, propose measurable improvement, and defend a correct position with evidence. Unresolved material conflict uses the bounded deliberation and escalation protocol.

## Virtual Governance Meetings

`VIRTUAL_MEETING_CATALOG.json` defines recurring evidence-led virtual meetings. Each meeting must produce decisions, owners, due dates, risks, assumptions, and escalations; it is not a conversational status ritual. The meeting graph dynamically loads all participant role instructions and produces an auditable meeting record.

## Concept Mapping

- Automotive SPICE contributes outcome-oriented processes, work-product evidence, configuration, quality assurance, and objective assessment.
- SAFe contributes portfolio alignment, business ownership, value flow, and Lean governance.
- Scrum contributes clear accountabilities for value, delivery, and team effectiveness; feature teams remain cross-functional and self-managing.
- ISO 19011 contributes evidence-based and independent audit principles.
- ISO 37301 contributes a proportionate compliance-management lifecycle: obligations, risk, controls, monitoring, review, and improvement.
