# Operating Update Cadence

## Purpose

Keep Triton lean, current, and evidence-led without allowing automatic changes to roles, prompts, skills, MCP servers, models, or controls.

## Cadence

| Cadence | Review | Required output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly | Legal, regulatory, data-protection, public-affairs, and security-source watch for active jurisdictions and exposed assets | Dated watch record, signal classification, and impact or no-material-change decision |
| Per delivery cycle | Import approved aggregated KPI and lesson exports from product workspaces | Evidence record and actionable improvement candidates |
| Monthly | Marketing/Sales, procurement/product operations, financial controls, model quality, and capability health | Decisions, owners, dates, exceptions |
| Quarterly | Role/skill/process portfolio and MCP/model technology review | Keep, trial, revise, retire, or escalate decision |
| Event-triggered | Security issue, regulatory change, major quality failure, customer claim, control failure, supplier failure, or model regression | Containment, impact assessment, corrective action, re-test |

## Change Control

1. Detect through KPIs, lessons, external-source watch, or an escalation.
2. Record source, reason, affected roles, expected benefit, risk, cost, data implications, and rollback plan.
3. Place new roles, skills, prompts, MCP configurations, or model candidates in Intake/Trial; no direct promotion to canonical use.
4. Test against representative, data-safe cases and compare quality, traceability, latency, cost, and failure rate with the incumbent.
5. The Role Skill Evolution Manager proposes the decision; independent Quality, Security, Legal, Privacy, or Finance reviewers join where relevant.
6. Promote only with a versioned decision record and a rollback route. Remove or disable failed trials.

## Technology Watch Boundaries

MCP and model reviews prefer maintained, open-source or no-cost capabilities where they meet the need. Updates are recommendations and controlled trials, never unattended installations, credential changes, data sharing, or replacement of a working capability.
