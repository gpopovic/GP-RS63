# Triton Business Model Portfolio

## Strategic Objective

Build a Germany-led, India-enabled technology business that wins on reliable outcomes, transparent delivery, and repeatable economics rather than low hourly rates alone.

## Recommended Offer Ladder

| Offer | Customer value | Revenue pattern | Why it scales | Primary risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paid discovery and solution blueprint | Reduce decision and implementation uncertainty | Fixed, short engagement | Reusable assessment method; converts qualified prospects | Giving away discovery work for free |
| Productised implementation package | Deliver a defined outcome with fixed boundaries | Fixed price and milestone billing | Reusable architecture, playbooks, and acceptance criteria | Hidden custom scope |
| Managed product/delivery team | Continuous improvement and predictable capacity | Monthly retainer with capacity and outcome boundaries | Recurring revenue; stable DE–IN delivery cadence | Becoming an unbounded staff-augmentation service |
| White-label engineering partnership | Give German agencies/system integrators dependable back-end capacity | Retainer or defined work packages | Uses partner sales channels; avoids building every lead channel from zero | Margin and customer relationship controlled by the partner |
| Vertical accelerator | Reuse a validated component, workflow, or integration for one target segment | Licence, setup, support, or implementation package | Higher margin and shorter delivery once evidence proves repeatability | Building a product before demand is proven |

## Portfolio Rule

Start with paid discovery plus a productised implementation offer. Add managed delivery for customers with a proven continuous need. Use white-label selectively for capacity utilisation and references. Invest in a vertical accelerator only after repeated customer demand exposes a stable, reusable pattern.

## Offer Quality Gate

An offer is active only when it has an ideal customer profile, measurable outcome, scope and exclusions, delivery blueprint, price logic, cost model, acceptance evidence, risk register, and a named owner.

## Core Commercial KPIs

| Area | KPI |
| --- | --- |
| Demand | Qualified-pipeline coverage, win rate, sales-cycle duration, offer-to-project conversion |
| Economics | Gross margin, contribution margin, cost-to-serve, utilisation, forecast variance |
| Cash | Days to invoice, days sales outstanding, milestone collection rate, unbilled work |
| Repeatability | Customisation ratio, delivery rework, time-to-value, renewal rate, recurring-revenue share |
| Risk | Customer/partner concentration, scope-change ratio, dependency concentration, payment-at-risk |

## Decision Cadence

- Weekly: pipeline quality, proposal risk, capacity, and cash blockers.
- Monthly: offer economics, delivery variance, and market signals.
- Quarterly: portfolio continue/grow/redesign/retire decisions backed by the Commercial Strategy Auditor.
