# Commercial Pricing Policy

## Purpose

Set a traceable commercial price before any customer offer is released. The policy protects Triton's ability to deliver quality work sustainably while allowing intentional, controlled customer-entry pricing.

## Price architecture

Every material offer shall contain an internal Commercial Brief with all five layers below. Only customer-safe price and scope information may enter the customer document.

| Layer | Purpose | Customer visibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Market reference | Comparable onshore, nearshore, offshore and specialist supplier rates; a negotiation anchor, not a claim about competitors | No |
| Target price | Preferred price for the defined scope, risk and service level | Yes, as applicable |
| Relationship price | Deliberate adjustment for an established customer with evidenced payment, scope and collaboration history | Yes, net price only |
| Introductory price | One-off, tightly bounded first-work-package commercial adjustment | Yes, net price or a labelled commercial adjustment |
| Stop line | Minimum viable price after direct cost, approved operating allocation and risk reserve | No |

## Initial controlled thresholds

Until Triton has at least six closed, cost-reconciled projects in the relevant delivery class, use these **provisional contribution-margin thresholds** after direct delivery cost, approved operating allocation and delivery-risk reserve:

| Zone | Contribution margin | Decision |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Target | 32% or higher | Normal approval path |
| Healthy | 30% to below 32% | Commercial Manager approval |
| Relationship / introductory | 26.4% to below 30% | Founder plus Finance Controller approval; documented reason and expiry |
| Below stop line | Below 26.4% | Do not send; change price, scope, staffing, risk allocation or decline |

The Finance and Risk Controller shall recalibrate these thresholds quarterly from actual cost, utilisation, sales effort, payment delay, rework, claims and reserve consumption. A planning benchmark is never an approved rate card.

## Customer segmentation and introductory offers

- An established-customer adjustment requires evidence of reliable payment, controlled scope/change behaviour, repeatable delivery collaboration and a recorded reason. Relationship status is not an automatic discount.
- An introductory offer may be used only once per new customer, for one defined work package and a stated validity period. It must not become the implied rate card for later work.
- The customer wording may state: *"For the first, clearly bounded work package, Triton is offering a one-time introductory commercial arrangement. It applies only to the agreed scope, period and named team; subsequent work is priced under the applicable rate card."*
- Never disclose internal cost, margin, stop line, capacity distress, or wording such as "this is our limit".

## Mandatory commercial controls

Before `SEND`, record: source and date of market references; quote version; scope; assumptions; real rate card; staffing and subcontractor cost; reserve; payment terms; currency/tax treatment; validity; liability/change treatment; target and actual contribution zone; approver; and pricing expiry.

For external delivery partners, a final price may not rely on a salary benchmark or an assumed local rate alone. Procurement must obtain or explicitly simulate comparable supplier responses that separate: individual delivery rate; intermediary/broker mark-up; replacement cover; management/quality charge; currency; tax; payment terms; minimum commitment; validity; and cancellation/change exposure. The Commercial Brief shall show low, base and adverse total-cost scenarios. A simulated quote is a planning input only and must be replaced by a real, dated quote before a real `SEND` decision.

For time-and-materials work, early-warning control shall be measured against the approved forecast baseline and ceiling, not accidentally against a starting price that already triggers the warning. The Commercial Brief must state both thresholds explicitly.

## Value Evidence and Target Costing

A proposal may compare price with a customer outcome or credible alternative only when the value case records the customer, decision context, baseline, time horizon, units, uncertainty, evidence source, value recipient, and what portion Triton can reasonably influence. Avoided loss, willingness to pay, and the highest imaginable customer benefit are scenarios, not a proven price ceiling. Customer vulnerability, fear, sunk cost, or unsupported loss claims must not be used as leverage.

For an approved offer, target cost may be calculated as the approved net price less the approved contribution objective and required reserves. If the feasible delivery estimate exceeds target cost, the team must change scope, method, service level, allocation, price, or decline the work. It may not compress safety, quality, privacy, legal duties, worker conditions, acceptance evidence, or contingency merely to force the target.

A requested price concession triggers an explicit comparison of price, scope, service, risk allocation, payment terms, capacity value, and relationship evidence. No concession is automatic, but neither is a proportional scope cut universally required. Any change remains subject to the Commercial Brief, stop line, contract authority, and independent review.

## KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Win rate by price zone | Won offers / submitted offers by Target, Healthy and Introductory zone | Commercial and Pricing Manager |
| Realised contribution variance | Actual contribution margin minus approved margin | Finance and Risk Controller |
| Introductory-to-repeat conversion | Introductory customers receiving profitable follow-on work / introductory customers | Account Director |
| Discount leakage | Follow-on work billed below intended rate-card zone without approval | Commercial Strategy Auditor |
| Days sales outstanding / payment deviation | Actual collection timing versus agreed term | Finance and Risk Controller |

## Independence and review

The Commercial Strategy Auditor independently tests the math, evidence, customer classification and any exception. The author of a price may not audit or approve the same exception. The policy is a controlled configuration item and changes require recorded founder approval.
