# Daily Founder Brief Protocol

## Purpose

Give the founder one concise, evidence-backed daily operating view without creating a second project source of truth or replacing accountable human decisions.

## Cadence and ownership

The daily heartbeat is scheduled for 09:00 Europe/Budapest in the founder's Triton task. Pepper, the Executive Steward and Operations Guard, leads the operational view. Jarvis, the Chief Engineer and Systems Architect, leads the technical-operating view. The founder decides priorities and material commitments.

## Required brief structure

1. **Executive state:** only material changes since the prior brief, or an explicit statement that none is evidenced.
2. **Pepper — meeting horizon:** confirmed meetings for today, the next-seven-day outlook, new controlled role requests that are not yet scheduled, preparation/evidence needed, founder participation or decisions, conflicts, and source gaps. Put `IMMEDIATE` and `TODAY` requests first.
3. **Pepper — operating view:** top priorities, material capacity-allocation conflicts, critical/near-critical or dependency-unlocking blockers, aging work, decisions due, control/legal/financial/communication risks, owners and due points. Keep protected obligations separate from relative economic priority.
4. **Jarvis — technical view:** architecture, configuration, toolchain, MCP/plugin, model-routing, automation, quality, and evidence risks.
5. **Founder decisions needed today:** only when a decision is actually required; each item names options, evidence, accountable owner, deadline, and consequence of delay.
6. **Next review point:** the smallest next evidence-gathering or decision step.

## Evidence and boundaries

- Separate facts, assumptions, recommendations, and unresolved questions.
- For calendar information, query only an explicitly authorised connected calendar. Keep confirmed calendar events distinct from unscheduled role requests. Inspect full event details only when preparation requires them.
- Read meeting requests only from the approved ALM or controlled outage fallback. A role request is not a scheduled meeting and not founder approval.
- If the authorised calendar, ALM, or controlled meeting-request source cannot be read, state `SOURCE UNAVAILABLE`; never infer that no meeting or request exists.
- Read only controlled Company configuration and explicitly authorised project-control records. Never scan credentials, `.env` files, raw media, or project workspaces merely to populate a brief.
- Refer to external product/project work through approved status, evidence identifiers, or sanitised learning exports; never make it a second backlog in Triton.
- Pepper and Jarvis may recommend, coordinate, and escalate. They may not self-approve releases, legal positions, payments, contracts, provider/model activation, permits, safety, structural work, procurement, or acceptance.
- Keep the brief under 500 German words. Silence low-value noise; surface material blockers and missing evidence early.
- The founder may explicitly choose a reduced-interaction mode for the current brief. In that mode, present one reversible next step, preserve the right to stop, and defer nonessential choices. Never infer energy, health, stress, or psychological state from activity, response time, telemetry, or tone; never lower a protected control.

## Escalation

An immediate escalation supersedes the routine brief for material security, privacy, safety, legal, delivery, or continuity risk. The escalation names severity, known facts, uncertainty, containment status, accountable owner, and decision deadline.
